         RE: Cabrillo II Apartments – Letter of Inquiry

         The California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) recently
         became aware that the affordable housing units located at the Cabrillo II Apartments, in
         the City of Ventura, may no longer be affordable. The purpose of this letter is to inquire
         about the current status of the affordable units located at the property; and if the rental
         subsidies have been terminated; ensure that required State Preservation Noticing Law
         Requirements have been fulfilled under California Government Code sections 65863.10
         and 65863.11.

         Under State Preservation Noticing Law, owners of government-assisted developments
         must provide “notices” of intent to terminate rental subsidies or restrictions at 36, 12,
         and 6 months to all affected tenants and to affected public agencies (Gov. Code §
         65863.10, sub. (b), (c), and (e)(2)). In addition, owners of government-assisted
         developments cannot terminate subsidy contracts; prepay a federally-assisted
         mortgage; or discontinue use restrictions without first providing an exclusive "notice of
         opportunity to submit an offer to purchase" to each identified qualified entity (Gov. Code
         § 65863.11, subd. (g)).

         HCD is responsible for implementing the State Preservation Notice Law, found in
         California Government Code sections 65863.10 and 65863.11. HCD requests a
         response to this inquiry within 30 days from the date of this letter. If the rental subsidies
         were terminated, and the required notices were properly issued, please provide copies
         to HCD by mail or email to Preservation@hcd.ca.gov. If notices were not issued, the
         Owner must issue the above notices to comply with the requirements of Government
         Code sections 65863.10 and 65863.11. HCD is charged with the duty to monitor
         compliance with these sections and refer violations to the Attorney General for the
         appropriate enforcement action.
