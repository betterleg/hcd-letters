          RE: City of Santa Monica – Enacted Senate Bill 79 Transit-Oriented Development
              Ordinance Findings Letter

          Thank you for submitting the City of Santa Monica’s (City) enacted Senate Bill (SB) 79
          Ordinance No. 2861 (Ordinance) and supporting documents to temporarily exclude the
          following sites from Government Code section 65912.157 until one year following the
          adoption of the seventh revision of the housing element:

               •    Sites that permit density and residential floor area ratios at no less than 50
                    percent of the minimum standards under Section 65912.157, subdivision (a), 1
                    and
               •    Sites in a transit-oriented development (TOD) zone in which at least 33 percent
                    of sites in the TOD zone permit density and residential floor area ratio no less
                    than 50 percent of the standards specified under Section 65912.157, subdivision
                    (a) and which includes sites with densities that cumulatively allow for at least 75
                    percent of the aggregate density for the TOD zone specified under Section
                    65912.157, 2 and
               •    Sites with a historic resource designation as of January 1, 2025, on a local
                    register. 3

          Pursuant to Government Code section 65912.160, subdivision (d)(3)(A), this letter
          consists of the California Department of Housing and Community Development’s (HCD)
          findings that the Ordinance is in substantial compliance with SB 79 as of the date of
          this letter.

      Findings

      According to Ordinance No. 2861 and the included documents submitted by the
      City (see Attachment A - Table 1), Section 9.06.20, subsections A, B and C of
      the Ordinance (Exclusion of Eligible Sites from Increased Development
      Standards) identify the criteria where a parcel is temporary excluded from
      Government Code Section 65912.157 until one year following the adoption of the
      seventh revision of the housing element. Section 9.06.030 of the Ordinance
      identifies the Director or designee to issue and update maps identifying eligible
      sites meeting the criteria as set forth in Section 9.06.20. 4

      Conclusion

      Provided the submitted documentation and assertions by the City are otherwise
      complete and accurate, HCD finds that all the requirements have been met for enacting
      an ordinance that is substantially compliant with SB 79 pursuant to Government Code
      section 65912.160, subdivision (c)(1). HCD reserves the right to request additional
      information to monitor ongoing substantial compliance with SB 79 and may update its
      findings should new information come to light. In addition, the Southern California
      Association of Government (SCAG) SB 79 map shall have a rebuttable presumption of
      validity for use by project applicants and local governments. 5

      HCD appreciates the cooperation of the City of Santa Monica’s staff during the course
      of this review. If you have any questions or need additional information, please contact
      Xjvirr Thomas at Xjvirr.Thomas@hcd.ca.gov. HCD is committed to assisting the City in
      addressing all statutory requirements of SB 79.
