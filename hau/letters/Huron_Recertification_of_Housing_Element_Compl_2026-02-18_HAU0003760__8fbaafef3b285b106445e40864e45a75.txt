         RE: City of Huron – Successful Completion of Corrective Action Letter

         Dear Virginia Peñaloza:

         Thank you for submitting Ordinance No. 2026-0002 to the California Department of
         Housing and Community Development (HCD), received for review on January 22, 2026
         in response to HCD’s May 16, 2025 Corrective Action Letter.

         The May 16, 2025 letter found that the City had failed to implement the required rezone
         actions in Program 6 (Use of Sites in Previous Cycles). HCD provided the City of Huron
         (City) until June 16, 2025 to send a written response to these findings. In response, the
         City submitted Ordinances No. 2025-0013, No. 25-01 and Resolution No. 2025-001.
         While this documentation addressed some requirements of Housing Element Law,
         revisions were needed to ensure by-right processing of sites identified in previous
         housing elements pursuant to Government Code section 65583.2, subdivision (c).

         HCD is pleased to find that the City has satisfied the requirements described in HCD's
         Corrective Action Letter by implementing required rezoning actions in Program 6
         through Resolution No. 2026-0002. Therefore, the City’s housing element remains in
         compliance with Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq).

         HCD looks forward to continuing to collaborate with the City in meeting the
         commitments in the housing element. Please feel free to contact Reid Miller, of our staff,
         at Reid.Miller@hcd.ca.gov with any questions or additional information.

         Sincerely,
