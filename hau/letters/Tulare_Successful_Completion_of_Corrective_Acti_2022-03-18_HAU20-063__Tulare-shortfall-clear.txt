         RE: Housing Element Shortfall Rezoning Status

         Dear Marc Mondell:

         Thank you for submitting Ordinance No. 18-13 (Ordinance) adopted January 15, 2019. In
         addition to reviewing the Ordinance, HCD considered comments from Central California
         Legal Services pursuant to Government Code section 65585, subdivision (c). Pursuant
         to Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i), the California Department of
         Housing and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         HCD is pleased to find the City of Tulare (City) has satisfied the requirements described
         in HCD’s May 26, 2016, May 7, 2018, June 27, 2018, and August 8, 2018 review letters
         to rezone sufficient sites to accommodate the identified 4th cycle planning period’s
         unaccommodated need of 969 units affordable to lower-income households. In addition,
         rezone actions accommodate the City’s 5th cycle unaccommodated need of 1,010 units
         affordable to lower-income households. As a result, HCD now finds the April 27, 2016
         adopted element to be in substantial compliance with State Housing Element Law
         (Article 10.6 of the Government Code).

         HCD continues to monitor implementation of housing element programs A-4
         (Infrastructure Capacity) and B-5 (Priority Sewer and Water Service for Affordable
         Housing). The City should continue to emphasize the development of infrastructure,
         including supplying clean water and wastewater services, to support housing in
         disadvantaged areas of the City, within unincorporated islands, and within the City’s
         sphere of influence.

         The City must monitor and report on the results of housing element programs through
         the annual progress report (APR), required pursuant to Government Code section
         65400. Please be aware that Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i), grants
         HCD authority to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it
         determines is inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law.
         This includes failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD
