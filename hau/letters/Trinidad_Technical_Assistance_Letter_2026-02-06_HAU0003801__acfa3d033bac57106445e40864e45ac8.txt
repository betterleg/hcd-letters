         Dear Trever Parker:

         The purpose of this letter is to provide technical assistance regarding the
         implementation of the City of Trinidad’s (City) 6th Cycle Housing Element programs
         pursuant to Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i). On July 25. 2025, the
         California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) sent a Letter of
         Inquiry to the City regarding the completion of past due Housing Element
         implementation programs. The City responded on September 16, 2025, informing HCD
         that Programs HI-1 (Amend Zoning Ordinance), HI-2 (Low Barrier Navigation Centers),
         and H-14 (Multifamily Permitting) have not yet been completed according to the timeline
         established in the City’s adopted Housing Element.

         HCD requests that the City provide an update on the status and timeline of
         implementation for the following:
         •    Program HI-1 (Amend Zoning Ordinance): The City should provide the relevant
              documentation to HCD upon California Coastal Commission (CCC) approval of
              zoning code amendments pertaining to manufactured housing and mobile homes,
              Single-Room Occupancy (SRO) units, transitional and supportive housing, employee
              housing, reasonable accommodations, and residential care facilities. Additionally, as
              stated in HCD’s prior review, the City should provide a timeline for the CCC approval
              of a separate Local Coastal Plan (LCP) update to include the Accessory Dwelling
              Unit (ADU) ordinance. The City confirmed on June 27, 2025, that the City was
              expecting to resubmit the ADU ordinance to CCC in December 2025.

              Furthermore, regardless of potential constraints, the City must provide a timeline to
              immediately adopt an ordinance that specifies how State Density Bonus Law (SDBL)
              will be implemented locally pursuant to Government Code Sections 65915-65918.
              HCD understands that an additional LCP amendment will likely need to be approved
              by the CCC, however HCD will provide a sample SDBL ordinance to the City under
              a separate cover.

•   Program HI-2 (Low Barrier Navigation Centers): The City must provide an immediate
    timeline (e.g., six months) for the adoption and CCC approval of zoning that is
    appropriate to accommodate Low Barrier Navigation Centers pursuant to
    Government Code Section 65660. According to AB 101, low-barrier navigation
    centers must be a by-right permitted use in zoning districts that permit multifamily
    and mixed uses, including nonresidential zones that permit multifamily uses.
•   Program HI-14 (Multifamily Permitting): HCD understands the City requires a
    Conditional Use Permit (CUP) in zones permitting multifamily development. The City
    confirmed on June 27, 2025, that it is discussing an administrative permit process for
    multifamily residential uses with the CCC. The City must provide a timeline to
    replace the conditional use permit for multifamily housing with a design review
    process that promotes objectivity and approval certainty. Additionally, the City
    should describe what modifications to the local design review standards are being
    considered and provide a timeline for when those updates will be adopted.

As a reminder, HCD found the local Housing Element in substantial compliance with
Housing Element Law on May 12, 2020, based in part on the City’s commitment to
complete Programs according to timeline commitments. Furthermore, Government
Code section 65585, subdivision (i), grants HCD authority to review any action or failure
to act by a local government that it determines is inconsistent with an adopted housing
element or Housing Element Law. This includes failure to implement program actions
included in the housing element. Failure to implement actions in Housing Element
Programs by the statutory deadline means the City’s housing element will no longer
substantially comply with Housing Element Law, and HCD may revoke its finding of
substantial compliance.

HCD requests that the City provide a timeline for the implementation of the programs
mentioned above. If necessary, documentation for completed programs should be
transmitted to HCD. HCD will review the documentation and issue correspondence
before taking any action authorized by Government Code section 65585, subdivisions
(i) and (j). Such action may include issuance of written findings (Corrective Action
Letter) and revoking HCD’s finding that the City’s housing element is in substantial
compliance with Housing Element Law and/or referral to the California Office of the
Attorney General.

HCD provides the City until March 6, 2026, to provide a written response to these
findings. If you have questions or need additional information, please contact
Anthony Errichetto at Anthony.Errichetto@hcd.ca.gov.
