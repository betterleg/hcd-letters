         RE: County of Ventura – Successful Completion of Housing Element Rezones

         Dear Dave Ward:

         The California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) received a
         request from the County of Ventura (County) to confirm that the County has satisfied the
         housing element rezoning actions in Program H in response to HCD’s March 17, 2025
         Letter of Inquiry. HCD is pleased to confirm that the County has satisfied the
         requirements described in HCD's Letter of Inquiry by implementing required rezoning
         actions in Program H through Policy HE-2.7 and Ordinance No.4651. Therefore, the
         City’s housing element remains in compliance with Housing Element Law (Gov. Code,
         § 65580 et seq).

         The March 17, 2025 letter requested a status update on the County’s housing element
         implementation Program H to allow by-right processing of sites identified in previous
         housing elements pursuant to Government Code section 65583.2, subdivision (c). HCD
         provided the County until April 17, 2025 to send a written response to the letter. The
         County submitted a response to HCD on March 27, 2025. Based on the County’s
         written response, and conversations with County staff, HCD found that the County had
         a policy in place to allow by-right processing of sites identified in previous housing
         elements through housing element Policy HE-2.7.

         In addition, the County committed to adopting an amendment to the municipal code to
         codify this policy. On September 19, 2025 the County completed the amendment
         through adoption of Ordinance No. 4651. HCD determined this ordinance met the
         requirements of Housing Element Law via email on October 31, 2025.

         HCD looks forward to continuing to collaborate with the County in meeting the
         commitments in the housing element. Please feel free to contact Reid Miller, of our staff,
         at Reid.Miller@hcd.ca.gov with any questions or additional information.
