          RE: City of Glendale – Enacted Senate Bill 79 Transit-Oriented Development
              Ordinance Findings Letter

          Thank you for submitting the City of Glendale’s (City) enacted Senate Bill (SB) 79
          Urgency Ordinance 1 and supporting documents, adopted under Urgency Ordinance
          No. 6053, to temporarily exclude from the standards and provisions of SB 79 for one
          year following the adoption of the seventh revision of the housing element:

               •    All sites within a very high fire hazard severity zone, as determined by the
                    Department of Forestry and Fire Protection pursuant to Section 51178, or within
                    the state responsibility area, as defined in Section 4102 of the Public Resources
                    Code per Government Code section 65912.161, subdivision (b)(1)(D), and
               •    Certain sites on the City’s local historic register as of January 1, 2025, per
                    Government Code section 65912.161, subdivision (b)(1)(F).

          Pursuant to Government Code section 65912.160, subdivision (d)(3)(A), this letter
          consists of the California Department of Housing and Community Development’s (HCD)
          findings that the Ordinance is in substantial compliance with SB 79 as of the date of
          this letter.

      Conclusion

      Provided the submitted documentation and assertions by the City are otherwise
      complete and accurate, HCD finds that all the requirements have been met for enacting
      an ordinance that is substantially compliant with SB 79 pursuant to Government Code
      section 65912.160, subdivision (c)(1). HCD reserves the right to request additional
      information to monitor ongoing substantial compliance with SB 79 and may update its
      findings should new information come to light. In addition, the Southern California
      Association of Government (SCAG) SB 79 map shall have a rebuttable presumption of
      validity for use by project applicants and local governments. 4

      HCD appreciates the cooperation of the City of Glendale’s staff during the course of this

      Xjvirr Thomas at Xjvirr.Thomas@hcd.ca.gov. HCD is committed to assisting the City in
      addressing all statutory requirements of SB 79.
