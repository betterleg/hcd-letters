RE: City of Culver City – Enacted Senate Bill 79 Ordinance – Review Letter

Thank you for submitting the City of Culver City’s (City) adopted and enacted
Ordinance 2026-007 amending Title 17 (Zoning Code) of the Culver City Municipal
Code to amend Chapter 17.210 (Residential Zoning Districts), Chapter 17.220 (Mixed
Use Zoning Districts), and Chapter 17.400 (Standards for Specific Land Uses) to
implement Senate Bill (SB) 79 within the City.

This letter consists of the California Housing Department of Housing and Community
Development’s (HCD) written findings pursuant to Government Code Section
65912.160, subdivision (d)(3) of SB 79 that the Ordinance is in substantial
compliance with SB 79 as of the date of this letter.

Findings

According to Ordinance 2026-007 and the included documents submitted by the City
(detailed in Attachment A - Table 1 below), the Ordinance applies to properties located
within one-half mile of a designated transit-oriented development stop within the City
The added Chapter 17.400, Section 17.400.116 (Transit-Oriented Housing
Development Projects) would incorporate the standards laid out in Government Code
Section 65912.157 and additional objective development and design standards.
Additionally, the Ordinance identifies sites that are excluded from SB 79 prior to one
year following the adoption of the seventh revision of the housing element because
these sites are designated on the Culver City’s local historic register as of January 1,
2025. 1

Conclusion

Provided the submitted documentation and assertions by the City are complete and
accurate, HCD finds that all the requirements have been met for adopting an ordinance
compliant with SB 79.
