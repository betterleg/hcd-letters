         RE: City of Beverly Hills Senate Bill 79 (SB 79) Site Eligibility – Letter of
             Technical Assistance

         The California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) received an
         inquiry regarding four parcels located at 211-217 South Hamilton Drive in the City of
         Beverly Hills (City). The purpose of this letter is to provide technical assistance
         regarding the determination of a housing development project site for purposes of
         applying the requirements and provisions under Government Code section 65912.157.

         Background

         HCD understands the four adjacent parcels of land are approximately 5,200 square feet
         each. Three of the parcels each contain a duplex, which are subject to local rent
         control1, and the fourth parcel contains a single-family home. SB 79 provides that a
         proposed development shall not be located on a “site containing more than two units
         where the development would require the demolition of housing that is subject to any
         form of rent or price control through a public entity’s valid exercise of its police power
         that has been occupied by tenants within the past seven years.”2

         For determining whether a “site” contains “more than two units” pursuant to
         Government Code section 65912.157, subdivision (h)(1), does the term “site” refer
         to each individual parcel or all parcels containing the proposed housing
         development project?

HCD previously provided technical assistance that a “site” or “project site” is the
parcel(s) containing the proposed housing development project. 3 Therefore, if a housing
development project includes all four parcels located at 211-217 South Hamilton Drive,
the site may not eligible for the development standards and provisions under
Government Code section 65912.157 if the housing development project would require
the demolition of the duplexes since the site would contain six units (i.e., more than two
units) that are subject to a form of rent or price control through a public entity.

If the applicant chooses to submit four separate housing development projects, one for
each parcel, then each site may be eligible for the development standards and
provisions under Government Code section 65912.157 because each site would not
contain more than two units that are subject to a form of rent or price control. 4

Conclusion

In summary, the application of Government Code section 65912.157, subdivision (h)(1)
provisions for the demolition of rent or price-controlled units depend on the parcel(s)
contained in the proposed housing development project.

HCD oversees compliance with SB 79 5, among other state housing laws. Accordingly,
HCD may review local government actions to determine consistency with these laws. If
you have questions or need additional technical assistance, please contact
