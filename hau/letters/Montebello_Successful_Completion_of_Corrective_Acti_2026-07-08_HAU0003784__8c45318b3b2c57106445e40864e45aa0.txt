         RE: City of Montebello – Successful Completion of Corrective Action Letter
         Dear Raul Alvarez:
         Thank you for submitting Ordinance No. 2474 Ordinance No. 2487, Ordinance No. 2488
         and Ordinance No. 2490 to the California Department of Housing and Community
         Development (HCD), received for review on May 14, 2026 in response to HCD’s
         October 17, 2025 Corrective Action Letter and April 7, 2026 Notice of Potential
         Violation. Additional supporting documentation was also received by HCD for review on
         June 18, 2026.
         The October 17, 2025 letter found that the City had failed to implement the required
         rezone actions in Program 6 (Adequate Sites to Accommodate the RHNA) and
         Program 11(Objective Design Standards, Approval By-Right, Administrative
         Approvals). The April 7, 2026 letter further clarified that the City was required to rezone
         all sites it committed to rezoning in its adopted housing element, or HCD would revoke
         its determination that the City’s housing element was in compliance with State Housing
         Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq).
         HCD is pleased to find that the City has satisfied the requirements described in HCD's
         Corrective Action Letter by implementing required rezoning actions in Program 6 and
         Program 11 through Ordinance No. 2474, Ordinance No. 2487, Ordinance No. 2488
         and Ordinance No. 2490. Therefore, the City’s housing element remains in compliance
         with State Housing Element Law.
         HCD looks forward to continuing to collaborate with the City in meeting the
         commitments in the housing element. Please feel free to contact Reid Miller, of our staff,
         at Reid.Miller@hcd.ca.gov with any questions or additional information.
         Sincerely,




         Melinda Coy
         Housing Accountability Unit Chief
