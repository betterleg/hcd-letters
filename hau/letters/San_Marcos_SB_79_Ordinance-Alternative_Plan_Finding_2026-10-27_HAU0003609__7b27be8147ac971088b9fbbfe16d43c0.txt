      Findings

      HCD reviewed the documents submitted by the City (see Attachment A - Table 1 City of
      San Marcos Submittal Documents) and finds that revisions will be necessary to
      substantially comply with the requirements of Government Code section 65912.161.
      Specifically:

      1. Section 20.280.040 (Eligible Sites). Subsection A states:

             “Parcels located within a transit-oriented development zone and zoned for
             residential use (R-1-20, R-1-10, R-1-7.5, R-2, R-3-6, R-3-10 Zones, and R-MHP),
             mixed use development (MU-1 and MU-2 Zones), commercial use (C and NC
             Zones), or within a Specific Plan Area (SPA) containing a residential, mixed-use,
             or commercial land use district shall be eligible for a transit-oriented housing
             development project pursuant to this chapter, subject to the applicable
             development standards of the transit-oriented development zone[.]”

      Government Code section 65912.157, subdivision (a) states (emphasis added): “A
      housing development project shall be an allowed use as a transit-oriented housing
      development on any site zoned for residential, mixed, or commercial development
      within one-half or one-quarter mile of a transit-oriented development stop….”
      HCD previously issued technical assistance explaining that “zoned for” means
      principally or conditionally permitted within a zone. 3

      In addition to the eligible sites identified in Section 20.280.040(A), there are further
      zoning districts that contain eligible SB 79 sites according to the City’s Planning Code
      because the zones principally or conditionally permit residential, mixed, or commercial
      development. HCD finds the following zones are within the City’s TOD zones and are
      not otherwise exempted or excluded from SB 79:

         •   Office Professional (OP) Zone: Section 20.220.040 principally or conditionally
             permits commercial development, including but not limited to lodging (hotels and
             motels), restaurants, financial institutions, internet-based sales, offices, and
             personal services, including fitness/health and instructional. 4 Additionally, San
             Marcos Municipal Code Section 20.220.020(C) states that the zone is intended for
             “the development of professional offices, business support, and personal
             services.”

      The City, in its resolutions adopting the Ordinances, included maps of the areas and

      TOD zones that are and are not covered by Section 65912.157.

      Conclusion

      The City has until Saturday, December 26, 2026 (60 days from this letter), to respond to
      the written findings. 10 Pursuant to Government Code section 65912.160, subdivision
      (d)(5), the City must consider the findings provided here and must either amend the
      Permanent Ordinance to comply with HCD’s findings or enact the Permanent Ordinance
      without changes. If the City chooses to enact the ordinance without changes, it must
      include findings in its resolution adopting the ordinance that explain the reasons the City
      believes the ordinance complies with Government Code section 65912.161 despite the
      findings provided by HCD.

      Please note, the City’s Permanent Ordinance, including applicable objective
      development standards, must not physically preclude a transit-oriented housing
      development that complies with the applicable development standards of Government
      Code section 65912.157. 11 HCD reserves the right to request additional information to
      monitor ongoing substantial compliance with SB 79 and may update its findings should
      new information come to light.

      HCD appreciates the cooperation of the City of San Marcos staff during the course of
      this review. If you have any questions or need additional information, please contact
      David Ying at david.ying@hcd.ca.gov. HCD is committed to assisting the City in
      addressing all statutory requirements of SB 79.
