         RE: City of Glendale’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Housing Element Implementation and
             No Net Loss Analysis

         Thank you for submitting the City of Glendale’s (City) various analyses and
         documentation received on July 17, 2026 along with additional supplemental
         information received on August 17, 2026 (Submittal). The Submittal relates to
         implementing the housing element (Program 1B: Maintain Adequate Sites Throughout
         the Planning Period) to maintain adequate sites to accommodate the regional housing
         need allocation (RHNA) and complying with No Net Loss Law (NNL). Pursuant to
         Government Code section 65585, the California Department of Housing and Community
         Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         HCD is pleased to find the Submittal adequately demonstrates the City continues to
         maintain adequate sites to accommodate the RHNA and complies with NNL pursuant to
         Government Code section 65863. For example, the Submittal demonstrates that recent
         pipeline projects will likely be available in the planning period and adequately cover the
         loss of capacity for the lower-income and moderate income RHNA.

         HCD appreciates the collaboration and effort provided throughout the review. HCD
         wishes the City success in implementing its housing element and looks forward to
         following its progress through the General Plan annual progress reports pursuant to
         Government Code section 65400. If HCD can provide assistance in implementing the
         housing element, please contact Rogelio Martinez, of our staff, at
         rogelio.martinez@hcd.ca.gov.

         Sincerely,
