         RE: City of Redlands – Successful Completion of Corrective Action Letter
         Dear Charles M. Duggan:
         Thank you for submitting Resolution No. 8688, 8689, 8690, 8700, Ordinance No. 2947,
         2983, and 2985 to the California Department of Housing and Community Development
         (HCD), which was received for review on August 4, 2025 in response to HCD’s
         June 2, 2025 Corrective Action Letter.

         The June 2, 2025 letter found that the City of Redlands (City) had failed to implement
         the required rezone actions in Program 1.1-1 (Regional Housing Needs Allocation
         (RHNA) Rezoning) and Program 1.1-4 (By-Right Approval of Projects with 20 Percent
         Affordable Units on “Reused” Site). HCD provided the City until July 2, 2025 to send a
         written response to these findings. The City responded on June 26, 2025 stating that all
         remaining rezones to meet the RHNA shortfall would be adopted July 15, 2025. On
         August 4, 2025, the City submitted adopted rezone resolutions and ordinances for
         HCD’s review. After review of the documentation, HCD reached out to the City on
         January 26, 2026 for clarification to ensure the submitted documentation met all
         statutory requirements. The City provided an email response on January 28, 2026.

         HCD is pleased to find that the City has satisfied the requirements described in HCD's
         Corrective Action Letter by implementing required rezoning actions in Program 1.1-1
         and Program 1.1-4 through Resolution No. 8688, 8689, 8690, 8700, Ordinance No.
         2947, 2983, and 2985. Therefore, the City’s housing element remains in compliance
         with Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq).

         HCD looks forward to continuing to collaborate with the City in meeting the
         commitments in the housing element. Please feel free to contact Reid Miller, of our staff,
         at Reid.Miller@hcd.ca.gov with any questions or additional information.

         Sincerely,
