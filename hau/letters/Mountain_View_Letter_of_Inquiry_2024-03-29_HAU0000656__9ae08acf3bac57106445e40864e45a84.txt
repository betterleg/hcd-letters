         RE: City of Mountain View, Gatekeeper Process – Letter of Inquiry

         The California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD)
         understands the City of Mountain View is working toward implementing the Gatekeeper
         process as outlined in its 6th Cycle Housing Element (2023 – 2031), specifically
         identified in Program Actions 1.3(f) and (g). As a continuation to HCD staff discussions
         with the City, the purpose of this letter is to obtain additional information on the next
         steps of revisions to the Gatekeeper process.

         Background

         In January 2024, HCD received an inquiry in its Housing and Accountability Unit (HAU)
         Portal regarding the City of Mountain View’s progress on outreach efforts and changes
         to the Gatekeeper process. The complainant expressed concerns that the City may be
         creating additional criteria to the Gatekeeper process that could create a cost burden on
         housing production.

         HCD is aware that the purpose of the City of Mountain View’s Gatekeeper process is to
         consider development proposals that advance community priorities that cannot
         otherwise be achieved under the City’s current General Plan and Zoning. According to
         the City’s adopted housing element related to the Gatekeeper process (Program Action
         1.3 Review and Update Ordinance and Precise Plan Residential Standards), it
         discusses the actions the City will develop during the planning period.

         Program Action 1.3:

                  (f) Identify additional Gatekeeper exemptions for residential projects based on
                  location, size, affordability, and other policy goals, and

       (g) Hold at least one Gatekeeper meeting per year, which may be limited to
       residential or residential mixed-use projects only, creating greater opportunities
       for project-specific rezonings.

Although there were initial discussions to include Qualifying Criteria and Community
Design Principles to the Gatekeeper process, HCD is aware that after the Study
Session held on January 23, 2024, the City decided to move forward under the current
Gatekeeper process and no additional criteria would be added at this time. HCD also
understands that the City is planning to accept applications between March – June 2024
and hold a Council Authorization hearing in the 3rd quarter of 2024 as well as conduct
outreach on the Gatekeeper updates.

Conclusion

To evaluate the progress on implementation on Program 1.3(g), HCD is requesting the
City provide the following information by April 29, 2024:

   •   A clear timeline/schedule for outreach on any future changes to the Gatekeeper
       process, and
   •   Information on the City’s efforts in identifying additional exemptions to the
       Gatekeeper process as described in Program Action 1.3(f).
