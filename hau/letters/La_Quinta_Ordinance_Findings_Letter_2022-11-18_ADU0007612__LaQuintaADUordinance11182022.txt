       RE: City of La Quinta – Follow-Up to ADU Ordinance Review
       Thank you for submitting the City of La Quinta’s (City) accessory dwelling unit (ADU)
       Ordinance No. 602 (Ordinance), adopted August 2, 2022, to the California Department of
       Housing and Community Development (HCD).

       The purpose of this letter is to advise the City that upon review, the section of the
       Ordinance that pertains to ADUs (Section 9.06.090) satisfactorily complies with current
       State ADU Law. HCD thanks the City for its efforts in conforming its local law with statute.
       As of this writing, HCD considers the matter of compliance with Government Code section
       65852.2, subdivision (h), closed.
       Please be advised that State ADU Law will change significantly as of January 1, 2023,
       with the chaptering of AB 2221 (Chapter 650, Statutes of 2022) and SB 897 (Chapter 664,
       Statutes of 2022). The City should review these pending changes and amend its ADU
       Ordinance accordingly.
       As always, HCD reminds the City that the State of California is in a housing crisis and the
       provision of housing for all Californians is a priority of the highest order. HCD stands ready
       to provide technical assistance and invites you to contact Michael McLaughlin, of our
       staff, at michael.mclaughlin@hcd.ca.gov or (916) 776-7773 should the City have any
       questions.
