         RE: County of Santa Barbara – Successful Completion of Corrective Action
             Letter

         Dear Lisa Plowman:

         Thank you for submitting Ordinance No. 5206, 5207, 5208, and 5209 along with
         Resolution No. 24-92, 24-93, and 24-94 to the California Department of Housing and
         Community Development (HCD), received for review on May 9, 2024 in response to
         HCD’s March 1, 2024 Corrective Action Letter.

         The March 1, 2024 letter found that the County had failed to implement the required
         rezone actions in Program 1 (Adequate Sites for Regional Housing Needs Allocation
         (RHNA) and Monitoring of No Net Loss) and Program 2 (Use By-Right Approval). HCD
         provided the County until March 31, 2024 to send a written response to these findings.

         HCD is pleased to find that the County has satisfied the requirements described in
         HCD's Corrective Action Letter by implementing required rezoning actions in Programs
         1 and 2 through Ordinance No. 5206, 5207, 5208, 5209, and Resolution No. 24-92, 24-
         93 and 24-94. Therefore, the County’s housing element remains in compliance with
         Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq).

         HCD looks forward to continuing to collaborate with the County in meeting the
         commitments in the housing element. Please feel free to contact Reid Miller, of our staff,
         at Reid.Miller@hcd.ca.gov with any questions or additional information.

         Sincerely,




         Melinda Coy
