          RE: City of Bellflower – Successful Completion of Corrective Action Letter

          Dear Elizabeth Oba:

          Thank you for submitting Ordinance No. 1460 to the California Department of Housing
          and Community Development (HCD), adopted September 14, 2026, and received for
          review on September 16, 2026, in response to HCD’s March 18, 2026, Corrective
          Action Letter.

          The March 18, 2026, letter found that the City of Bellflower (City) had failed to
          implement the required rezone actions in Program 27 (Ensure Adequate Sites to
          Accommodate the Regional Housing Needs Allocation (RHNA)) to rezone adequate
          sites to satisfy its RHNA shortfall. 1

          HCD is pleased to find that the City has satisfied the requirements described in HCD's
          Corrective Action Letter by implementing required rezoning actions in Program 27
          through the adoption of Ordinance No. 1460. Therefore, the City’s housing element
          remains in compliance with State Housing Element Law. 2

          HCD looks forward to continuing to collaborate with the City in meeting the
          commitments in the housing element. Please feel free to contact Stephanie Chan, of our
          staff, at Stephanie.Chan@hcd.ca.gov with any questions or additional information.

          Sincerely,




          Fidel Herrera
          Section Chief, Housing Element Implementation
