         RE: Emergency Shelter Zoning Status

         Dear Brian Peters:

         The Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) is responsible for
         administering state housing element law (Article 10.6 of the Government Code), including the
         review of local housing elements for compliance. The purpose of this letter is to inquire about
         the current status of housing element implementation.
         HCD found the county’s housing element in compliance based on, among other things,
         Implementation Program 2 - Zoning Ordinance Review and Amendment which committed to
         zone and permit emergency shelters without discretionary action within one year of housing
         element adoption pursuant to Government Code (GC) Section 65583(a)(4)(A). HCD requests a
         status on the implementation of program actions. Please see the attached form to assist your
         response. Submit the response within 30 days via email to Jess.Negrete@hcd.ca.gov.
         In 2017, the Governor signed the 2017 Legislative Housing Package, which provides a renewed
         focus on housing, one of the most basic needs for every Californian. Chapter 370 Statutes of
         2017, Assembly Bill (AB) 72, is one of the bills that was passed. AB 72 expands and clarifies
         HCD’s enforcement authorities. HCD will review local government’s actions and inactions,
         including program actions committed within an adopted housing element, to determine
         consistency or inconsistency with state housing law. If HCD makes findings of inconsistency,
         housing element compliance may be revoked and additional actions may be taken, including
         referral to the Attorney General’s Office.
         Information regarding Government Code Section 65583(a)(4)(A) can be found on HCD’s
         website at: http://www.hcd.ca.gov/community-development/housing-element/housing-element-
         memos/docs/SB2-Memo050708.pdf. If you have questions or need additional information,
         contact Jess Negrete at (916) 263-7437.
