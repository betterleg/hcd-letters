         RE: Halter Haven Apartments – Letter of Inquiry

         The California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) is writing to
         inquire about the affordable units at Halter Haven Apartments (Property) located at
         21905 Powhattan Road in the City of Apple Valley. HCD recently became aware that
         the Property’s affordable units may no longer be affordable or are approaching the
         contract termination date. The purpose of this letter is to inquire about the current status
         of the Property’s affordable units. If the rental subsidies have been terminated, please
         confirm that all requirements under Preservation Noticing Law, as outlined in
         Government Code sections 65863.10 and 65863.11, have been met.

         Background

         On January 16, 2025, HCD contacted the Property representative to inquire about the
         affordability status of the units. During the phone call, the Property representative noted
         that the required notices had been sent to the required entities. However, HCD does not
         have record of receiving the required notices. Additionally, while the Property
         representative stated that the units have continued affordability under a voucher
         program, the representative was unwilling to provide additional details or
         documentation. As the listed owner of the Property, please provide an update to HCD of
         the current affordability status of the Property and provide any recorded documentation
         in fulfillment of Preservation Noticing Law under Government Code sections 65863.10
         and 65863.11.
      Connie Halter, Managing Partner

      Preservation Notice Law

      Owners of government-assisted developments cannot terminate subsidy contracts,
      prepay a federally assisted mortgage, or discontinue use restrictions without first
      providing an exclusive “Notice of Opportunity to Submit an Offer to Purchase” to
      qualified entities. 1

      Additionally, Three-Year Notices, Twelve-Month Notices, and Six- Additionally, Three-
      Year Notices, Twelve-Month Notices, and Six-Month Notices are required to be
      provided to affected tenants and prospective tenants prior to the anticipated date of the
      termination of a subsidy contract, the expiration of rental restrictions, or prepayment of
      an assisted housing development. 2

      HCD is responsible for implementing the Preservation Notice Law, found in Government
      Code sections 65863.10, 65863.11 and 65863.13 and has released templates for the
      notices required by Government Code sections 65863.10 and 65863.11, located on
      HCD’s website. 3

      Owners of multifamily residential developments that receive governmental assistance
      under programs defined in Government Code section 65863.10(a)(3) must register their
      properties and complete an annual certification of compliance with Preservation Notice
      Law. If a property is operated under a ground lease or similar structure, the title holder
      and the lessee are mutually responsible for the completion of the certification. 4 To
      certify the Property, find the California Housing Preservation Portal (CaHPP Portal) on
      HCD’s website.

      Next Steps

      HCD requests a written response to this inquiry within 30 days from the date of this
      letter. If the rental subsidies have been terminated and the required Notice of
      Opportunity to Submit an Offer to Purchase to qualified entities, Three-Month Notices,
      Twelve-Month Notices, and Six-Month Notices were properly issued, please provide
      copies to HCD by email to Preservation@hcd.ca.gov.
