         Dear John King:

         The California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) has reviewed
         the City of Paramount’s (City) obligations under the housing element and hereby issues its
         written findings that the City has failed to implement Programs 7 (Adequate Sites and
         Monitoring of No Net Loss (SB166)) and Program 9e (Zoning Amendments) to
         rezone adequate sites to satisfy its Regional Housing Needs Allocation (RHNA) shortfall
         and to allow by-right processing of sites identified in previous housing elements. 1

         State Housing Element Law

         HCD must review any action or failure to act by a City that it determines to be
         inconsistent with an adopted housing element or section 65583 generally, and it must
         issue written findings to the City accordingly. 2 HCD must give the City a reasonable
         time, no longer than 30 days, to respond to these findings. 3 If HCD does not receive a
         written response from the City within 30 days, or the response does not demonstrate
         that the program action has been implemented, then HCD will revoke its findings that
         the City’s housing element substantially complies with Housing Element Law. 4

         Additionally, HCD may notify the California Office of the Attorney General when a City
         takes actions that are inconsistent with an adopted housing element or Government
         Code sections 65583 and 65915, among other laws. 5

Findings

On October 7, 2022, HCD found the City’s housing element in substantial compliance with
Housing Element Law. HCD based its finding of substantial compliance on, among other
things, a commitment to implement housing element Programs 7 and 9e. These programs
are committed to rezone adequate sites to satisfy its RHNA shortfall pursuant to
Government code section 65583, subdivision (c)(1)(A), and to allow by-right processing of
sites identified in previous housing elements pursuant to Government Code section
65583.2, subdivision (c).

On March 17, 2025, HCD sent the City a Letter of Inquiry requesting an update on the
City’s progress implementing rezone actions on Programs 7 and 9e. The City provided a
written response on May 5, 2025 stating that the Paramount City Council adopted
Ordinance No. 1173 which finalized the zone change to North Paramount Gateway, to
address the City’s lower income RHNA shortfall. However, the Clearwater East Specific
Plan (renamed Clearwater Specific Plan) was not expected to be completed until February
of 2026. Upon review of the documentation received by the City, HCD determined that
without the adoption of the Clearwater Specific Plan, the City still has a lower income
RHNA capacity shortfall of approximately 59 units.

On October 6, 2025, HCD sent the City an email for clarification on several items
relating to the submitted rezone documentation. The City replied on October 27, 2025,
and HCD confirmed that, while the North Paramount Gateway Specific Plan zone
changes met the requirements of Housing Element Law, the City still had a shortfall of
lower-income RHNA. On December 29, 2025, HCD sent a follow-up email to receive an
update on the Clearwater Specific Plan’s timeline of adoption. On January 8, 2026, the
City responded via phone that the Paramount City Council will not adopt the plan until
late April or early May 2026.

Based on the timeline provided, HCD finds that the City has failed to implement this
required rezone program action within the statutorily required timeframe.

Next Steps

HCD requests that the City review this correspondence and provide a written response
to these findings within 30 days and no later than April 17, 2026. HCD will review and
consider the City’s written response before taking any action authorized by Government
Code section 65585, subdivisions (i) and (j). As noted below, such action could include
revoking HCD’s finding that the City’s housing element is in substantial compliance with
Housing Element Law and/or referral to the California Office of the Attorney General.
