          RE: City of Monterey – Successful Completion of Corrective Action Letter

          Dear Kimberly Cole:

          Thank you for submitting Ordinance No. 3722 to the California Department of Housing
          and Community Development (HCD), adopted August 18, 2026, and received for review
          on September 3, 2026, in response to HCD’s May 20, 2026, Corrective Action Letter.

          The May 20, 2026, letter found that the City of Monterey (City) had failed to implement
          the required rezone actions in Program 1-B (Multi-Family Residential Overlay
          Amendments) because the City had not adopted minimum density standards in the
          Multifamily Residential Overlay District to meet the statutorily required minimum of 20
          dwelling units per acre for sites being used to accommodate the City’s lower-income
          Regional Housing Needs Allocation (RHNA). 1

          HCD is pleased to find that the City has satisfied the requirements described in HCD's
          Corrective Action Letter by completing required rezoning actions in Program 1-B
          through Ordinance No. 3722. Therefore, the City’s housing element remains in
          compliance with State Housing Element Law. 2

          HCD looks forward to continuing to collaborate with the City in meeting the
          commitments in the housing element. Please feel free to contact Mary Milner, of our
          staff, at Mary.Milner@hcd.ca.gov with any questions or additional information.

          Sincerely,




          Fidel Herrera
          Section Chief, Housing Element Implementation
