      This letter consists of HCD’s written findings pursuant to Government Code section
      65912.160, subdivision (d)(3)(A) that the Ordinances are in substantial compliance
      with SB 79 as of the date of this letter.

      Findings

      According to the Ordinances and the included documents submitted by the City
      (detailed in Attachment A - Table 1), the Ordinances apply to properties located within
      one-half mile of a transit-oriented development (TOD) stop. Ordinances 5702 and 5704
      identify sites that are temporarily excluded from the applicability of SB 79 because they
      were designated as local historic resources on the Palo Alto Historic Resources
      Inventory as of January 1, 2025.

      Ordinance 5703 establishes a Transit Oriented Development (TOD) Combining District
      that covers all non-City-owned and non-historic resource sites, 3 and modifies
      development standards that would otherwise “preclude development of the densities
      and residential floor areas required for a site within the TOD Combining District to
      qualify under Government Code section, 65912.161, subd. (b)(1)(A).” 4 Unless the
      underlying zoning district would be more permissive, the TOD Combining District sets
      maximum floor area ratios at 50 percent of those specified under Government Code
      section 65912.157, subdivision (a) and adds additional objective development
      standards. 5 Additionally, the TOD Combining District does not have a maximum density
      within one-half mile of a TOD stop. Ordinance 5703 temporarily excludes these sites
      pursuant to Government Code section 65912.161, subdivision (b)(1)(A).

      Conclusion

      Provided the submitted documentation and assertions by the City are complete and
      accurate, HCD finds that all the requirements have been met for enacting an ordinance
      that is substantially compliant with SB 79 pursuant to Government Code section
      65912.160, subdivision (c)(1).

      Please note that the Ordinances, including applicable objective development standards,
      must not physically preclude a transit-oriented housing development that complies with
      the applicable development standards of Government Code section 65912.157, 6 as
      modified by the City pursuant to Government Code section 65912.161, subdivisions
      (b)(1)(A) and (b)(1)(F). HCD reserves the right to request additional information to
      monitor ongoing substantial compliance with SB 79 and may update its findings should
      new information come to light.
