          Thank you for submitting the City of South Pasadena’s (City) adopted and enacted
          Senate Bill (SB) 79 ordinances and supporting documents to temporarily exclude
          certain sites from the standards and provisions of SB 79 for one year following the
          adoption of the seventh revision of the housing element, including:

               •    Uncodified Ordinance 2414 to temporarily exclude certain sites with a historic
                    resource designated on the City’s local historic register as of January 1, 2025,
                    per Government Code section 65912.161, subdivision (b)(1)(F).
               •    Uncodified Urgency Ordinance 2418 1 to temporarily exclude certain sites that
                    permit density and residential floor area ratio (FAR) at no less than 50 percent of
                    the standards in Government Code Section 65912.157, subdivision (a), per
                    Government Code section 65912.161, subdivision (b)(1)(A). As of the date of this
                    letter, Ordinance 2418 has expired.
               •    Uncodified Ordinance 2419 to temporarily exclude certain sites that permit
                    density and residential FAR at no less than 50 percent of the standards in
                    Government Code Section 65912.157, subdivision (a), per Government Code
                    section 65912.161, subdivision (b)(1)(A).

          Pursuant to Government Code section 65912.160, subdivision (d)(3)(A), this letter
          consists of the California Department of Housing and Community Development’s (HCD)
          findings that the Ordinances are in substantial compliance with SB 79 as of the date
          of this letter.

Findings

According to the Ordinances and the documents submitted by the City (detailed in
Attachment A – Table 1), the Ordinances apply to properties within one-quarter mile of
the City’s Mission Meridian Metro A Line transit-oriented development (TOD) stop.
Ordinance 2414 identifies sites containing a historic resource designated on the City’s
local Inventory of Historic Resources as of January 1, 2025. Ordinance 2419 identifies
sites that permit density and residential FAR at no less than 50 percent of the standards
specified under Government Code section 65912.157, subdivision (a), including sites
that are zoned either Mixed-Use Core or Mixed-Use Core-50, which allow 50 and 70
dwelling units per acre, respectively, and are not subject to a maximum residential FAR.

Conclusion

Provided the submitted documentation and assertions by the City are otherwise
complete and accurate, HCD finds that all the requirements have been met for adopting
an ordinance that is substantially compliant with SB 79 pursuant to Government Code
section 65912.160, subdivision (c)(1). HCD reserves the right to request additional
information to monitor ongoing substantial compliance with SB 79 and may update its
findings should new information come to light.

HCD appreciates the cooperation of the City of South Pasadena staff during the course
of this review. If you have any questions or need additional information, please contact
Lisa Frank at lisa.frank@hcd.ca.gov. HCD is committed to assisting the City in
addressing all statutory requirements of SB 79.
