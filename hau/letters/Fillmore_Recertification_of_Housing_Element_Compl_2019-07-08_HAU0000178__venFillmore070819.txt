RE: Fillmore's 5th Cycle (2015-2023) Adopted Housing Element Update

Thank you for submitting Fillmore's housing element adopted March 26, 2019. The
Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) received the document on
April 9, 2019. Pursuant to Government Code, section 65585, subdivision (h), HCD is
reporting the results of its review.

On March 20, 2019, HCD found Fillmore's draft housing element would comply upon
adoption and submission to HCD, in accordance with Gov. Code section 65585, subd.
(g), contingent upon city approval of the 77 unit affordable housing development. As of
June 11, 2019, Fillmore has approved the development and taken several additional
actions to address community's housing needs. Further, HCD has received Fillmore's
Ordinance No. 18-896 on November 30, 2018 along with additional supplemental
information on December 5, 2018. The Ordinance verifies action completed pursuant to
Gov. Code section 65583, subd. (a)(4)(A) (zoning for emergency shelters) and
addresses the requirements described in HCD's May 7, and July 6, 2018 review letters.
As a result, HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in full compliance with
state housing element law (Article 10.6 of the Government Code).

Several federal, state and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, CalTrans Senate Bill (SB)
1 Sustainable Communities grants; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD's Affordable
Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and the SB 2 Planning grant as well
as ongoing SB 2 funding consider housing element compliance and/or annual
reporting requirements pursuant to Gov. Code section 65400. The city now meets
housing element requirements for these funding programs and could be eligible.
However, eligibility is also contingent on submission of the city's annual report pursuant
to Gov. Code Section 65400. For more information and assistance, please email
sbplanninggrant@hcd.ca.gov and APR@hcd.ca.gov. Once the annual progress report
is submitted, Fillmore will be eligible for the above programs.
David Rowlands, City Manager

HCD appreciates the dedication and hard work of the City of Fillmore staff, especially
Kevin McSweeney. His positive attitude and diligent effort is an asset to the
