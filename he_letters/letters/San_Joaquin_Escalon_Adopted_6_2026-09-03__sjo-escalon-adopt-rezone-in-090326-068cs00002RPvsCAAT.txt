         RE: City of Escalon’s 6th Cycle (2024-2032) Adopted Housing Element and Rezone

         Thank you for submitting the City of Escalon’s housing element that was adopted
         August 10, 2026 and received for review on August 11, 2026. In addition, the California
         Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) received Zoning
         Ordinance Amendment Number 606 and other materials (Zoning Documents) related to
         making sites with zoning available to accommodate the regional housing need
         allocation (RHNA). Pursuant to Government Code section 65585, the California
         Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of
         its review. In addition, HCD considered comments from Rand Paster Nelson, California
         Rural Legal Assistance (CRLA) and Bold Communities pursuant to Government Code
         section 65585, subdivision (c).

         On June 26, 2026, HCD determined that the revised draft Housing Element met the
         statutory requirements of State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq).
         However, the element could not be found in substantial compliance until the City
         adopted the housing element and completed all necessary rezones to accommodate
         the RHNA to meet statutory requirements pursuant to Government Code sections
         65583, subdivision (c)(1) and 65583.2, subdivision (c). Upon review of the Zoning
         Documents, HCD is pleased to find that the City has completed the rezoning actions
         necessary to comply with the applicable statutory requirements listed above. Further,
         the adopted housing element is substantially the same as the revised draft that HCD
         reviewed on June 26, 2026. As a result, the adopted element and Zoning Documents
         now substantially comply with State Housing Element Law as of the date of this letter.

         As a reminder, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of this, and
         all programs including, but not limited to, the following:

              •   Program 1-2 (Maintain Viable Sites)
              •   Program 2-1 (Zoning for a Variety of Housing Types)

The City must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

In addition, HCD urges the City to diligently implement all programs and, particularly,
Program 1-2 (Maintain Viable Sites) and Program 3-3 (Conservation of Affordable Units
and Mobile Homes). Program 1-2 contains essential commitment to promote multifamily
development on C-2 zoned sites and HCD encourages the City to consider objective
development standards as part of the implementation process. Further, in consideration
of comments from CRLA, HCD further urges the City to move up implementation
timelines related to conservation of mobile home parks and consider CRLA’s additional
comments on this housing submittal as part of the implementation process. HCD looks
forward to monitoring the progress in implementation of these and other programs and
cautions the City that failure of actions or actions inconsistent with these programs will
trigger a review of the housing element that may result in revocation of substantial
compliance.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City now meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.
