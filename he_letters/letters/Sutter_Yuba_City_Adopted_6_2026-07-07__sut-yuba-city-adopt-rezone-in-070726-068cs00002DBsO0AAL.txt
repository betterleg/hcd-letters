         RE: City of Yuba City’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Amendment to the Adopted Housing
             Element and Rezones

         Thank you for submitting the City of Yuba City’s (City) amendment to the housing
         element that was adopted May 5, 2026, and received for review on May 13, 2026. In
         addition, the California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD)
         received Ordinance Number 007-23, Resolution 23-055, X29 Overlay District and
         Development Services Department Policy (Rezoning Documents) intended to
         accommodate the regional housing need allocation (RHNA). Pursuant to Government
         Code section 65585, HCD is reporting the results of its review.

         On May 5, 2022, HCD found the City’s housing element in substantial compliance with
         State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq.), and as of this writing, the
         City continues to comply with State Housing Element Law. For example, this finding of
         substantial compliance was based on commitments to rezone and accommodate the
         RHNA pursuant to Government Code sections 65583, subdivision (c)(1), and 65583.2,
         subdivisions (c), (h), and (i). This adopted amendment does not impact HCD’s
         May 5, 2022, finding of substantial compliance. This finding is based on, among other
         items, completion of Programs H-C-6 (Residential By-Right for Developments with 20
         Percent Affordability) and H-C-7 (2013-2021 and 2021-2029 Regional Housing Need) to
         make zoning available to accommodate the RHNA and meet statutory requirements
         pursuant to Government Code sections 65583, subdivision (c)(1) and 65583.2,
         subdivisions (c), (h) and (i).

         Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs

              •   H-A-2: Accessory Dwelling Units
              •   H-A-3: Facilitate a Variety of Housing Types

The City must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through the
annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400. Please
be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority to
review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is inconsistent
with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes failure to
implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke housing
element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state law.
Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City continues to meet housing element requirements for these and other
funding sources.
HCD appreciates the cooperation and responsiveness the City’s housing element team
provided throughout the update and review. HCD wishes the City success in
implementing its housing element and looks forward to following its progress through
the general plan annual progress reports pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
If HCD can provide assistance in implementing the housing element, please contact
Irvin Saldana, of our staff, at Irvin.Saldana@hcd.ca.gov.
