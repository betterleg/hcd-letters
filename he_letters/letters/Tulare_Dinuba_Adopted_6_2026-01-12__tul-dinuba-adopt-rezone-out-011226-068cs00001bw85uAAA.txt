         Thank you for submitting the City of Dinuba’s (City) housing element that was adopted
         October 14, 2025 and received on November 13, 2025. In addition, the California

         documents including Ordinances 2024-04, and 2025-03 (Zoning Documents). These

         the regional housing need allocation (RHNA). Pursuant to Government Code section
         65585, HCD is reporting the results of its review.

         On August 29, 2025, HCD found the revised draft housing element met statutory
         requirements of State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq). However, the
         element could not be found in substantial compliance until the City adopted and
         completed the necessary rezones to accommodate its RHNA and satisfy applicable
         statutory requirements. The adopted housing element is substantially the same as the
         revised draft that was reviewed on August 29, 2025 and continues to meet statutory
         requirements. However, the City must still make findings regarding nonvacant sites and
         demonstrate the Zoning Documents meet all statutory requirements, as follows:

              •   Nonvacant Sites Findings: As of the most recent review, the City relies on
                  nonvacant sites to accommodate 50 percent or more of its lower-income RHNA.
                  As a result, the City must make findings, supported by substantial evidence and
                  included in a resolution, that the existing uses on these sites are not an
                  impediment to residential development and are likely to discontinue during the
                  planning period, pursuant to Government Code section 65583.2, subdivision
                  (g)(2). For additional information, please see HCD’s Sites Inventory Guidebook
                  (p. 26) at https://www.hcd.ca.gov/community-development/housing-
                  element/housing-element-
                  memos/docs/sites_inventory_memo_final06102020.pdf. In addition, HCD will
                  send a resolution template under separate cover.

   •   Rezoning: A jurisdiction that did not adopt a compliant housing element within 120
       days from the statutory deadline (December 31, 2023) must rezone sites (Program 2
       (Adequate Sites) and Program 3 (By-Right Approval)) within one year of the
       statutory deadline and meet requirements pursuant to Government Code sections
       65583, subdivision (c)(1)(A) and 65583.2, subdivisions (c), (h) and (i), as applicable.
       While the City has successfully completed Program 3 (By-Right Approval) to utilize
       sites from past planning periods toward the lower-income RHNA, Program 2
       (Adequate Sites) has not been completed to meet all statutory requirements. As this
       year has passed, the housing element is out of compliance and will remain out of
       compliance until the rezoning has been completed and submitted to HCD to meet all
       statutory requirements. Specifically, to address all statutory requirements, for sites
       intended to accommodate a shortfall of capacity for the lower-income RHNA, zoning
       must ministerially (without discretionary action) permit housing developments in
       which at least 20 percent of the units are affordable to lower-income households.
       In addition, the City must demonstrate that corresponding changes to the general
       plan land use map were adopted consistent with recent zoning changes. Once
       the City amends the rezoning to meet all statutory requirements, a resolution or
       ordinance and other pertinent documentation should be transmitted to HCD.
       HCD will review the documentation and issue correspondence identifying the
       updated status of the City’s housing element compliance.

The element will substantially comply with State Housing Element Law once rezones
are completed to meet all statutory requirements, findings have been adopted and
documentation has been submitted to and reviewed by HCD to comply with the above
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65585.

above and submit documentation to HCD to regain housing element compliance.

Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City will meet housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation at:
https://www.lci.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.

HCD appreciates the hard work and commitment of the housing element team
