          Dear James Lewis:

          RE: Pismo Beach’s 5th Cycle (2014-2019) Adopted Housing Element

          Thank you for submitting the City of Pismo Beach’s housing element adopted
          November 19, 2019 and received for review on December 5, 2019 along with
          additional documentation on January 14 and February 24, 2020. Pursuant to
          Government Code section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of
          Housing and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

          HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in full compliance with state
          housing element law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code). The adopted element addresses
          the statutory requirements described in HCD’s May 17, 2019 review. HCD’s finding
          was based on, among other reasons, the rezoning of two sites to accommodate the
          Regional Housing Need Allocation for lower-income households for the past and
          current planning periods.

          For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
          element adoption. HCD reminds the City of Pismo Beach to consider timing provisions
          and welcomes the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the
          Technical Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
          http://opr.ca.gov/docs/OPR_Appendix_C_final.pdf and
          http://opr.ca.gov/docs/Final_6.26.15.pdf.

          Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
          compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, CalTrans Senate Bill (SB)
          1 Sustainable Communities grants; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s Affordable
          Housing and Sustainable Communities program; the SB 2 Planning Grants as well as
          ongoing SB 2 funding (Permanent Local Housing Allocation) consider housing element
          compliance and/or annual reporting requirements pursuant to Government Code
          section 65400. With housing element compliance, Pismo Beach now meets the
          housing element requirements for these funding sources.
James Lewis, City Manager
