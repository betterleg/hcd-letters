          RE: City of La Quinta (2022-2029) Adopted Housing Element

          Thank you for submitting the City of La Quinta’s (City) housing element adopted
          September 20, 2022 and received for review on September 29, 2022. The iterative
          changes related to the June 13, 2022, submittal and subsequent revisions to the
          adopted element are authorized by Resolution No. 2022-033. Pursuant to Government
          Code section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing and
          Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

          HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in full compliance with State
          Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code). The adopted element, including
          modifications, addresses the statutory requirements described in HCD’s June 29, 2022,
          review.

          Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs
          including, but not limited to, the following:

               •   Program H-2.3.b: Mixed Use in the Highway 111 Corridor: This is a program that
                   could potentially yield 100-300 new units by reducing development fees and
                   density bonuses and limiting provisions for the inclusion of affordable housing
                   units in Mixed Use projects. This would allow the City to take advantage of the
                   high density residential permitted in the Mixed Use overlay within the Highway
                   111 Corridor area in the future.
               •   Program H-3.3.b: Encourage Lot Consolidation: This is a program that
                   encourage consolidation of small lots in the Village Commercial area and
                   working with private land owners and developers to make way for multi-family
                   projects with incentives from the City. These may include fee deferral or
                   reductions, parking requirement reduction, and development standard relief.

          •   Program H-4.4.a: Housing Condition Survey and Monitoring: This program
              would complete an inventory of housing conditions to properly target Code
              Compliance and rehabilitation resources. Inventory should be done on a regular
              basis and focus on older neighborhoods.

       The City must monitor and report on the results of this and other programs through the
       annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400. Please
       be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority to
       review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
       inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
       failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may
       revoke housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply
       with state law.

       Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
       compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
       (SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
       Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
       Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
       requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
       element, the City of La Quinta meets housing element requirements for these and
       other funding sources.

       For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
       element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
       the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
       Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
       https://www.opr.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.

       HCD appreciates the time and effort the housing element update and review team has
       provided throughout the course of the housing element review. HCD wishes the City of
       La Quinta success in implementing its housing element and looks forward to following
       its progress through the General Plan annual progress reports pursuant to Government
       Code section 65400. If HCD can provide assistance in implementing the housing
       element, please contact Tristan Lanza, of our staff, at tristan.lanza@hcd.ca.gov.
