         RE: San Luis Obispo’s 6th Cycle (2020-2028) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of San Luis Obispo’s (City) housing element adopted
         November 17, 2020 and received for review on July 23, 2021. Pursuant to Government
         Code section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing and
         Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element including the minor, administrative
         changes allowed per adopted Resolution No. 11190, as described in your July 23, 2021
         correspondence, in full compliance with State Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the
         Gov. Code). The adopted element addresses the statutory requirements described in
         HCD’s March 22, 2021 review. HCD’s finding was based on, among other reasons,
         successful implementation of Program 3.8 to provide resources to support the housing
         authority, and local housing agencies, purchase and manage at-risk units, Program 5.5
         to allow mixed-use development without a use permit in certain zones, and Programs
         and Policies under Goal 6 to facilitate the production of housing.

         For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
         element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes the
         opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical Advisories
         issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
         http://opr.ca.gov/docs/OPR_Appendix_C_final.pdf and
         http://opr.ca.gov/docs/Final_6.26.15.pdf.

         Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
         compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
         (SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
         Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
         Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual
         reporting requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a
