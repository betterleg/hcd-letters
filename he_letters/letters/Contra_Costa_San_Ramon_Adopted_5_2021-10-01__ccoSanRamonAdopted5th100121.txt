      RE: San Ramon’s 5th Cycle (2015-2023) Adopted Housing Element Update

      Thank you for submitting the City of San Ramon’s (City) housing element adopted
      November 27, 2018 and received for review on July 16, 2021. Pursuant to Government
      Code section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing and
      Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.
      HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in full compliance with State Housing
      Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code). The adopted element was found to be
      substantially the same as the revised draft element that HCD’s October 24, 2018 review
      determined met statutory requirements.

      With a compliant housing element, the City meets housing element requirements for
      several federal, state, and regional funding programs that consider housing element
      compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
      (SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
      Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
      Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance.

      HCD appreciates the effort Debbie Chamberlain, Community Development Director and
      Ryan Driscoll, Associate Planner provided throughout the housing element review. HCD
      wishes the City success in implementing its housing element and looks forward to
      following its annual progress reports pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
      Please contact Hillary Prasad at Hillary.Prasad@hcd.ca.gov for any assistance.
