         RE: City of Lakewood’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of Lakewood’s (City) housing element adopted on
         August 9, 2022 and received for review on August 12, 2022, along with technical
         modifications authorized by Resolution Number 2022-60. Pursuant to Government
         Code section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing and
         Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in full compliance with State
         Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code). The adopted element, including
         modifications, addresses the statutory requirements described in HCD’s
         February 18, 2022 review.

         Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs
         including, but not limited to, the following:

             •    Program A-1 (Availability of Adequate Sites for New Housing for Regional
                  Housing Needs Allocation (RHNA)): This program commits to a rezoning that
                  includes a mixed-use ordinance on at least 125 acres, increase multi-family
                  densities to 30-50 du/ac for at least 83.5 acres. These rezones have also
                  committed to be targeted in the highest resource areas. In addition, this program
                  commits to modifying or establishing development standards to facilitate
                  maximum densities as well as by-right approvals for developments with 20
                  percent affordability.
             •    Program A-4 (By Right Approval of Projects with 20 Percent Affordable Units):
                  Pursuant to Government Code section 65583.2, subdivision (c), Lakewood will
                  amend the Zoning Ordinance to require by-right approval of housing
                  development that includes 20 percent of the units as housing affordable to lower-
                  income households, on sites being used to meet the 6th Cycle RHNA that
                  represent “reuse sites” previously identified in the 4th and 5th cycles Housing
                  Element. Pursuant to Government Code section 65583.2, subdivisions (c) and (i),

       zoning will allow at least 30 units per acre and permit multifamily uses by-right.
   •   Program A-5 (Minimum Density): This program has committed to amend the
       Zoning Ordinance to establish minimum densities to ensure that parcels are
       developed close to their maximum densities.
   •   Program A-6 (Inclusionary Housing Ordinance): This program commits to
       conduct a feasibility study of an inclusionary ordinance and amend the zoning
       ordinance to establish inclusionary housing requirements requiring new
       developments reserve up to 15 percent of total units for lower and moderate-
       income households.
   •   Program A-7 (Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) Tracking and Monitoring): This
       program commits to monitoring the production and affordability of ADUs. In the
       event of a shortfall, Lakewood has committed to adopt another ADU program
       within six months to facilitate additional ADU production.
   •   Program A-11 (Evaluation of Programs): This program commits to conducting a
       comprehensive evaluation of implementation and effectiveness in 2025 as a mid-
       cycle assessment. The evaluation, among other items, will address actions to
       affirmatively further fair housing and zoning on identified sites, including
       multifamily zoning on sites with residential uses. The City will further commit to
       adjusting programs as necessary based on the results of this evaluation,
       including but not limited to rezoning, increasing allowable densities, incentives,
       modifications of development standards and other actions.
   •   Program B-3 (Density Bonus Updates): This program commits to update the
       density bonus ordinance to meet current state requirements.
   •   Program B-5 (Development Standard Review and Revision): This program
       commits to study and revise development standards to facilitate maximum
       densities, specifically amending: Open space requirements, minimum parking
       standards, height limits, lot coverage for elderly and disabled housing and
       accessibility requirements.

The City must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

For your information, Program A-1 (Availability of Adequate Sites for New Housing for
RHNA) must be completed no later than three years and 120 days from the statutory
deadline (February 12, 2025). Otherwise, the local government’s housing element will
no longer comply with State Housing Element Law, and HCD may revoke its finding of
substantial compliance pursuant to Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i).

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City now meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.
