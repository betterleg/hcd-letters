         RE: City of Colton’s 6th Cycle (2023-2031) Adopted Housing Element and Rezone

         Thank you for submitting the City of Colton’s (City) Ordinance No. O-05-26, Ordinance
         No. 2002, Resolution No. R-14-26, which were adopted February 17, 2026 and received
         on March 10, 2026; Ordinance No. 0-08-25, which was adopted On August 5, 2025 and
         received for review on April 2, 2026; and Policy Document No. 2025-003, adopted
         December 1, 2025 and received for review April 28, 2026. These documents were
         submitted to verify completed actions in the City’s housing element Program 10 (Mixed-
         Use Development), Program 11 (Rezoning Sites), and Program 18 (By-Right Approval
         of Projects with 20 Percent Affordable Units on “Reused” Regional Housing Needs
         Allocation (RHNA) Sites). Pursuant to Government Code section 65585, the California
         Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of
         its review.

         HCD’s December 11, 2023 review found that, while the City’s adopted housing element
         met most of the statutory requirements of Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, Article
         10.6), it could not be found in full compliance until the City had completed rezoning
         actions in Programs 10,11, and 18 to address the shortfall of sites to accommodate the
         RHNA pursuant to Assembly Bill 1398 (Chapter 358, Statutes of 2021). HCD is pleased
         to find that the above resolution and ordinances complete all requirements in Programs
         10,11, and 18 and Government Code section 65583.2, subdivisions (c), (h) and (i). The
         City’s housing element, which was adopted January 4, 2022, with technical
         modifications completed on February 17, 2023 and September 20, 2023 as permitted
         under Resolution No. R-04-22, is therefore in substantial compliance with State Housing
         Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq) as May 7, 2026.

         Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs
         including but not limited to the following:

       supportive housing and low barrier navigation centers to ensure consistency with
       state law.
   •   Program 26 (Group Homes): This program commits to encourage development
       of housing options for persons with special needs. This includes creating a
       reasonable accommodation process consistent with state law and modifying
       restrictive Conditional Use Permit requirements for group homes of seven or
       more persons.
   •   Program 37 (Subdivision of Large Lots): This program commits to, among other
       things, annually coordinate with property owners of sites larger than ten acres
       throughout the City to provide incentives and streamlining mechanisms to
       accelerate production of affordable housing in the City.


The City must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City now meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation at:
https://www.lci.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.

HCD appreciates the efforts of the housing element update team during the housing
element update and review. HCD wishes the City success in implementing its housing
element and looks forward to following its progress through the General Plan annual
progress reports pursuant to Government Code section 65400. If HCD can provide
assistance in implementing the housing element, please contact Reid Miller, of our staff,
at Reid.Miller@hcd.ca.gov.
