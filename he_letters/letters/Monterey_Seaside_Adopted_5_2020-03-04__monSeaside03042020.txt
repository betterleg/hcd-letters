          RE: Seaside’s 5th Cycle (2015-2023) Adopted Housing Element

          Thank you for submitting Seaside’s housing element adopted December 19, 2019 and
          received for review on January 7, 2020. Pursuant to Government Code section 65585,
          subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing and Community Development
          (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

          On October 24, 2019, HCD found the City of Seaside’s draft housing element would
          comply with state housing element law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code) when it was
          adopted, submitted to, and approved by HCD, in accordance with Government Code
          section 65585, subdivision (g). HCD also found Seaside is subject to Government
          Code Section 65588(e)(4) to adopt housing element update within 120 calendar days
          from the statutory due date (December 31, 2015 for AMBAG localities). Since the city
          has not submitted a 4-year update and the due date has passed (December 31, 2019),
          the element no longer complies with housing element law. The element will comply
          with housing element law once the city has submitted a draft, considered HCD's
          findings and adopted a compliant 4-year update pursuant to Government Code Section
          65588.

          For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
          element adoption. HCD reminds the City of Seaside to consider timing provisions and
          welcomes the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the
          Technical Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
          http://opr.ca.gov/docs/OPR_Appendix_C_final.pdf and
          http://opr.ca.gov/docs/Final_6.26.15.pdf.

          Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
          compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. Housing element compliance is a
          threshold criteria to receive SB 2 Planning Grant funding. HCD received the City’s SB 2
