         Dear Tyler Summersett:

         RE: City of Sonora’s 7th Cycle (2024-2029) Adopted Housing Element and Rezone

         Thank you for submitting the City of Sonora’s (City) housing element that was adopted
         October 20, 2025 and received for review on November 20, 2025. In addition, the
         California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) received draft
         zoning text changes intended to make prior identified sites available to accommodate
         the regional housing need allocation (RHNA). Pursuant to Government Code section
         65585, HCD is reporting the results of its review.

         On June 9, 2025, HCD found the revised draft housing element met statutory
         requirements of State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq). The adopted
         element is substantially the same as the revised draft and as a result, the element
         continues to meet statutory requirements of State Housing Element Law. However, as
         noted in the prior review, pursuant to Assembly Bill 1398 (Chapter 358, Statutes of
         2021), as the City did not adopt a compliant housing element within 120 days of the
         statutory deadline (June 30, 2024), Program 3.1 (Adequate Sites), to make prior
         identifies sites available, must be completed no later than one year from the statutory
         deadline pursuant to Government Code sections 65583, subdivision (c) and 65583.2,
         subdivision (c). As this year has passed and Program 3.1 (Adequate Sites) has not
         been completed, the housing element will remain out of compliance until the rezoning
         has been completed and submitted to HCD to meet all statutory requirements. HCD has
         reviewed the draft zoning text changes and, if adopted, the zoning changes would meet
         statutory requirements. Once the City completes the rezoning, a copy of the resolution
         or ordinance should be transmitted to HCD. HCD will review the documentation and
         issue correspondence updating the status of the City’s housing element compliance.
Tyler Summersett, Director

As a reminder, the City’s 7th cycle housing element was due June 30, 2024. As of
today, the City has not completed the housing element process for the 7th cycle. HCD
encourages the City to expeditiously complete its rezoning and submit documentation to
HCD to regain housing element compliance.

Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs
including but not limited to the following:

   •   Program 1.2 (Neighborhood Improvements with Housing)
   •   Program 2.1 (Pursue Grant Funding)
   •   Program 2.3 (Support Infill Development)
   •   Program 2.4 (Accessory Dwelling Units)
   •   Program 2.5 (Incentives and Assistance)
   •   Program 3.1 (Adequate Sites)
   •   Program 5.3 (Zoning Code Amendments)
   •   Program 7.1 (Homelessness)
   •   Program 7.3 (Reasonable Accommodation)
   •   Program 7.5 (Family Definition)

The City must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City will meet housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation at:
https://www.lci.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.
