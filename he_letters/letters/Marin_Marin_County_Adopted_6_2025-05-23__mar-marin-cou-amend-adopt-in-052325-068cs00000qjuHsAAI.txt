         RE: County of Marin’s 6th Cycle (2023-2031) Adopted Amendment to the Housing
             Element

         Thank you for submitting the County of Marin’s (County) amendment to the housing
         element that was adopted on March 4, 2025 and received for review on May 5, 2025.
         Pursuant to Government Code section 65585, the California Department of Housing
         and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the result of its review regarding the
         draft amendment to the housing element.

         On June 19, 2023, HCD found the County’s housing element in substantial compliance
         with State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et. seq.) and that compliance
         status continued through this amendment process. On September 12, 2024, HCD also
         found that draft amendments would not negatively impact the finding of substantial
         compliance . However, the revised draft amendments received on January 9, 2025,
         substantially differed from the draft amendments reviewed on September 12, 2024 and,
         if adopted, HCD’s March 3, 2025 review found the revised draft amendment would
         negatively impact HCD’s prior finding of substantial compliance.

         At the same time, HCD’s March 3, 2025 review also found, with suggested
         modifications, the revised draft amendment would not negatively impact HCD’s finding
         of substantial compliance with State Housing Element Law once the revised draft
         amendment has been adopted, submitted, and reviewed by HCD pursuant to
         Government Code section 65585. HCD has reviewed the adopted amendments to the
         housing element and finds those amendments incorporate the suggested modifications
         in the March 3, 2025 review. As a result, HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing
         element and amendments continue to substantially comply with State Housing Element
         Law. This finding of substantial compliance has not changed since the June 19, 2023
         finding of substantial compliance with State Housing Element Law.
Sarah Jones, Director

The County must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Affordable Housing and Sustainable
Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent Local Housing Allocation consider
housing element compliance and/or annual reporting requirements pursuant to
Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing element, the County
continues to meet housing element requirements for these and other funding sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the County to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation:
https://www.lci.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.
