             Amendment

         Thank you for submitting the City of Ontario’s (City) housing element amendment adopted
         June 16, 2026, and received for review on July 31, 2026. Pursuant to Government Code
         section 65585, the California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD)
         is reporting the results of its review.

         On October 7, 2022, HCD found the City’s housing element in substantial compliance with
         State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq) and as of this writing, the City
         continues to comply with State Housing Element Law. On April 1, 2026, the City submitted
         draft housing element amendments to HCD for review, and HCD issued a review letter on
         May 21, 2026 finding that, while the draft amendment continued to meet most statutory
         requirements, it might impact HCD’s prior finding of substantial compliance if it were
         adopted without required revisions.

         HCD is pleased to find the City has addressed the requirements that were described in
         HCD’s May 21, 2026 review. Specifically, the City sufficiently updated its Affirmatively
         Furthering Fair Housing (AFFH) analysis in relation to housing element sites inventory.
         As a result, the adopted amendment to the housing element continues to substantially
         comply with State Housing Element Law as of the date of this letter. This finding of
         substantial compliance has remained unchanged since HCD’s October 7, 2022 review.

         Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs,
         including but not limited to the following:

The City must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or Housing Element Law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Affordable Housing and Sustainable
Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent Local Housing Allocation consider
housing element compliance and/or annual reporting requirements pursuant to
Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing element, the City continues
to meet housing element requirements for these and other funding sources.

HCD appreciates the efforts and cooperation provided throughout both the housing
element update and review process. HCD wishes the City success in implementing its
housing element and looks forward to following its progress through the general plan
annual progress reports required pursuant to Government Code section 65400. If HCD
can provide assistance in implementing the housing element, please contact
Reid Miller, of our staff, at Reid.Miller@hcd.ca.gov.
