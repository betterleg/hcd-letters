         RE: City of West Covina’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Rezone Review

         Dear Paulina Morales:

         Thank you for submitting the City of West Covina’s (City) Ordinance 2525 related to
         rezoning to making prior identified sites available to accommodate the lower-income
         regional housing need allocation (RHNA). The Ordinance was adopted March 18, 2025
         and received for review on April 24, 2025. Pursuant to Government Code section
         65585, the California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) is
         reporting the results of its review.

         On September 18, 2024, HCD found that the City’s adopted element met statutory
         requirements of State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq). Additionally,
         the City submitted Ordinance 2506 intended to demonstrate that the necessary
         rezoning was completed. However, HCD found that Ordinance 2506 did not meet all
         statutory requirements. As part of that review, the City submitted draft zoning
         amendments and HCD found that the draft amendments would comply with the
         statutory requirements once adopted, submitted to and approved by HCD, in
         accordance with Government Code section 65585.

         HCD is pleased to find the City has completed actions to address the requirements that
         were described in HCD’s September 18, 2024 review. Specifically, HCD finds that
         Ordinance Number 2525 meets all statutory requirements pursuant to Government
         Code section 65583.2, subdivision (c). As a result, the adopted element substantially
         complies with State Housing Element Law as of the date of this letter.

         Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs,
         including but not limited to the following:

              •   Program 1.2: Monitor and Preserve Affordable Housing
              •   Program 2.1: Acquisition and Rehabilitation
              •   Program 2.2: Support Production of Affordable Housing
              •   Program 3.1: Review and Update the Development Review Process

The City must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through the
annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400. Please
be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority to
review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is inconsistent
with an adopted housing element or Housing Element Law. This includes failure to
implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke housing
element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Affordable Housing and Sustainable
Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent Local Housing Allocation consider
housing element compliance and/or annual reporting requirements pursuant to
Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing element, the City now
meets housing element requirements for these and other funding sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation at:
https://www.lci.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.

HCD appreciates the efforts and cooperation provided throughout both the housing
element update and Ordinance adoption processes. HCD wishes the City success in
implementing its housing element and looks forward to following its progress through
the general plan annual progress reports required pursuant to Government Code
section 65400. If HCD can provide assistance in implementing the housing element,
please contact Margaret Scarpa, of our staff, at Margaret.Scarpa@hcd.ca.gov.
