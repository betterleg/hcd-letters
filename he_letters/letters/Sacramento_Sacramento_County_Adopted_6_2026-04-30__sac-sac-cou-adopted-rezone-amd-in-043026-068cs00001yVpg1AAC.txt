         RE: County of Sacramento’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Adopted Amendment to the
             Housing Element and Rezones

         Thank you for submitting the County of Sacramento’s (County) adopted amendment to
         the housing element that was received for review on March 2, 2026. In addition, the
         California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) received various
         resolutions, ordinances, proposed zoning changes and other planning documents
         (Zoning Documents) related to rezoning to accommodate the regional housing need
         allocation (RHNA). Pursuant to Government Code section 65585, the HCD is reporting
         the results of its review.

         On May 9, 2022, HCD found the County’s housing element in substantial compliance
         with State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq) and as of this writing, the
         County continues to comply with State Housing Element Law. Among other items, this
         finding of substantial compliance was based on commitments to rezone and
         accommodate the RHNA pursuant to Government Code sections 65583, subdivision
         (c)(1) and 65583.2, subdivisions (c), (h) and (i). On January 23, 2026, HCD reviewed
         the revised draft amendments and found that the revisions did not affect the
         May 9, 2022 finding of substantial compliance. The adopted amendments are
         substantially the same as the draft amendments reviewed on January 23, 2026. In
         addition, HCD has reviewed the Zoning Documents and finds the County complies with
         statutory requirements related to rezoning to accommodate the RHNA pursuant to
         Government Code sections 65583, subdivision (c)(1) and 65583.2, subdivisions (c), (h)
         and (i). As a result, the adopted housing element, its amendments and Zoning
         Documents continue to meet the requirements of State Housing Element Law. This
         finding of substantial compliance has remained unchanged since HCD’s May 9, 2022
         review.
Todd Smith, Planning Director

The County must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Affordable Housing and Sustainable
Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent Local Housing Allocation consider
housing element compliance and/or annual reporting requirements pursuant to
Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing element, the County
continues to meet housing element requirements for these and other funding sources.
