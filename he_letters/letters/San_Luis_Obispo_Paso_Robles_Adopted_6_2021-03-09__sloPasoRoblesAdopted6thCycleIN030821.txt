         RE: Review of Paso Robles’ 6th Cycle (2020-2028) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of Paso Robles (City) housing element adopted
         December 1, 2020 and received for review on December 9, 2020. Pursuant to
         Government Code section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing
         and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in full compliance with State
         Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code). The adopted element was found
         to be substantially the same as the revised draft element that HCD’s October 8, 2020
         review determined met statutory requirements.

         While the adopted housing element has been found in full compliance, HCD advises the
         City to make additional considerations for accessory dwelling units (ADUs) for this
         planning cycle. The element includes Program 3: Accessory Dwelling Units to monitor
         ADU applications and approvals annually and implement additional incentives as
         appropriate. In addition to monitoring production, this program should also monitor
         affordability. Also, additional actions, if necessary, should be taken in a timely manner
         (e.g., within 6 months). Further, if necessary, the degree of additional actions should be
         in stride with the degree of the gap in production and affordability. For example, if actual
         production and affordability of ADUs is far from anticipated trends, then rezoning or
         something similar would be an appropriate action. If actual production and affordability
         is near anticipated trends, then measures like outreach and marketing might be more
         appropriate. Finally, the City may wish to adjust its assumed rate of ADUs (45 ADUs per
         year) based on actual units permitted and affordability in the future for the purposes of
         demonstrating and monitoring adequate sites (Program 1: Adequate Sites) and
         maintaining adequate sites (Program 2: No Net Loss) pursuant to Government Code
         section 65863.
Warren Frace, Director

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City of Paso Robles to consider timing provisions
and welcomes the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the
Technical Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
http://opr.ca.gov/docs/OPR_Appendix_C_final.pdf and
http://opr.ca.gov/docs/Final_6.26.15.pdf.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City of Paso Robles meets housing element requirements for these and
other funding sources.

HCD appreciates the dedication and cooperation that the City’s housing element team
provided throughout the course of the housing element review. HCD wishes the City of
Paso Robles success in implementing its housing element and looks forward to
following its progress through the General Plan annual progress reports pursuant to
Government Code section 65400. Please contact Shawn Danino, of our staff, at
Shawn.Danino@hcd.ca.gov with any needed additional assistance.
