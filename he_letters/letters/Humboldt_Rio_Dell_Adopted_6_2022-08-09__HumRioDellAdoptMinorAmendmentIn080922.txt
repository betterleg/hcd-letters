         RE: Rio Dell’s Adopted Amendment to the Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting City of Rio Dell’s (City) amendment to the housing element
         adopted June 7, 2022 and received for review on June 16, 2022. The California
         Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) is required to review
         adopted housing elements and report findings to the locality pursuant to Government
         Code Section 65585(h).

         The City’s housing element was found in compliance on December 4, 2020. The
         adopted amendments to the land use and housing elements do not negatively impact
         the compliance status of the City’s housing element. As a result, HCD is pleased to find
         the adopted amendment to the housing element in full compliance with State housing
         Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code).

         HCD wishes the City success in implementing its housing element and looks forward to
         following its progress through the General Plan annual progress reports pursuant to
         Government Code Section 65400. If HCD can assist in implementing the housing
         element, please contact Mashal Ayobi, of our staff, at Mashal.Ayobi@hcd.ca.gov.


         Sincerely,
