A. Review and Revision

   Review the previous element to evaluate the appropriateness, effectiveness, and progress
   in implementation, and reflect the results of this review in the revised element. (Gov. Code,
   § 65588 (a) and (b).)

   The adopted element provided a program-by-program review to evaluate City's
   performance in addressing housing goals for most of the programs mentioned in HCD’s
   October 18, 2021 review. However, programs that still need information and evaluation
   include the following:

   Program 2 (Affordable Housing In-Lieu Funds) – Describe and evaluate the
   recommendations of the in-lieu fund study that were adopted and their appropriateness
   moving forward.

   Program 4 (Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers) – Include the number of referrals made to
   the Orange County Housing Authority in the previous housing element cycle.

D. Public Participation

   Local governments shall make a diligent effort to achieve public participation of all economic
   segments of the community in the development of the housing element, and the element shall
   describe this effort. (Gov. Code, § 65583, subd. (c)(8).)

   The element was revised to summarize public comments and describe how they were considered
   and incorporated into the element. The element must still describe how the City made diligent
   efforts to involve all economic segments of the community in the development of the housing
   element. While the element included a list of organizations, it was not clear whether those
   organizations provided feedback or whether the City engaged them further than notifying them of
   the process. Further, the prior review called for the application of additional methods for public
   outreach, particularly to include lower-income and special needs households and neighborhoods
   with higher concentrations of lower-income households.
