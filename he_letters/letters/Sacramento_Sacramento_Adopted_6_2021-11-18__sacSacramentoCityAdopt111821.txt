         RE: City of Sacramento’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of Sacramento’s (City) housing element adopted
         August 17, 2021 and received for review on August 20, 2021. The Department of
         Housing and Community Development (HCD) has also received correspondence
         related to affirmatively furthering fair housing on November 18, 2021. Pursuant to
         Government Code section 65585, subdivision (h), HCD is reporting the results of its
         review.

         Although the adopted housing element addresses most statutory requirements
         described in HCD’s June 7, 2021 review; revisions are necessary to comply with State
         Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code). Specifically, programs to
         affirmatively further fair housing (AFFH) must have metrics and milestones. At the same
         time, the City has proposed potential revisions in its November 18, 2021
         correspondence. These revisions are sufficient to address the remaining statutory
         requirements and the element will be in full compliance once the revisions are adopted
         into the housing element.

         Public participation in the development, adoption and implementation of the housing element
         is essential to effective housing planning. Throughout the housing element process, the City
         should continue to engage the community, including organizations that represent lower-
         income and special needs households, by making information regularly available and
         considering and incorporating comments where appropriate.

         Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
         compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
         (SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
         Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
         Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
         requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
