         RE: City of Dinuba’s 6th Cycle (2023-2031) Adopted Housing Element and Rezone

         Thank you for submitting the City of Dinuba’s (City) adopted housing element. The
         element was adopted on October 14, 2025 and supplemental findings adopted on
         January 13, 2026. The adopted element was received by the California Department of
         Housing and Community Development (HCD) on February 2, 2026. In addition, HCD
         received Resolution Numbers 2026-05 and 2026-08 along with Ordinances 2024-04
         and 2026-02 and technical corrections to the zoning map (Zoning Documents). These
         Zoning Documents were submitted to address statutory requirements and
         accommodate the regional housing need allocation (RHNA). Pursuant to Government
         Code section 65585, HCD is issuing the results of its review.

         On August 29, 2025, HCD determined that the revised draft Housing Element met the
         statutory requirements of State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq).
         However, the element could not be found in substantial compliance until the City made
         findings, supported by substantial evidence, that the existing uses on these sites are not
         an impediment to residential development and are likely to discontinue during the
         planning period and all necessary rezones to accommodate its RHNA are completed
         and meet statutory requirements pursuant to Government Code sections 65583,
         subdivision (c)(1) and 65583.2, subdivisions (c), (h) and (i). Upon review of Resolution
         Number 2026-05 and Zoning Documents, HCD is pleased to find that the City has made
         the appropriate findings and completed the rezoning actions necessary to comply with
         the applicable statutory requirements described above. Further, the adopted Housing
         Element is substantially the same as the revised draft reviewed on August 29, 2025. As
         a result, the adopted element and Zoning Documents now substantially comply with
         State Housing Element Law as of the date of this letter.
Luis Patlan, City Manager

The City must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City now meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation at:
https://www.lci.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.
