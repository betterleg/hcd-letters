         Thank you for submitting the County of San Joaquin’s (County) amendment to the
         housing element that was adopted June 30, 2026, and received for review on
         July 13, 2026. Pursuant to Government Code section 65585, the California Department
         of Housing and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         On August 22, 2025, HCD found the County’s housing element in substantial
         compliance with State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq.), and as of
         this writing, the County continues to comply with State Housing Element Law. For
         example, this finding of substantial compliance was based on Program 1-6 (RHNA
         Transfer Agreement with Mountain House), which requires the County to amend its
         sites inventory as appropriate upon a regional housing needs allocation (RHNA)
         transfer. The adopted amendment does not impact HCD’s August 22, 2025 finding of
         substantial compliance. This determination is based on, among other items, the
         completion of Program 1-6 and its requirement to amend the sites inventory following a
         RHNA transfer.

         Additionally, the County must continue timely and effective implementation of all

The County must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the County continues to meet housing element requirements for these and
other funding sources.

HCD appreciates the cooperation and responsiveness the County’s housing element
team provided throughout the update and review. HCD wishes the County success in
implementing its housing element and looks forward to following its progress through
the general plan annual progress reports pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
If HCD can provide assistance in implementing the housing element, please contact
Irvin Saldana, of our staff, at Irvin.Saldana@hcd.ca.gov.
