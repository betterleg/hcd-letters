         RE: 5th Cycle (2013-2021) Housing Element Notice of Noncompliance

         Dear Mr. Infanzon:

         The California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) received
         your letter dated September 23, 2020 in response to HCD’s August 24, 2020 Notice of
         Noncompliance and our subsequent September 21, 2020 meeting. HCD appreciates
         the challenges facing the City of Huntington Park (the City) in developing a strategy to
         accommodate new housing growth and supports the City’s formation of the ad hoc
         housing committee to tackle these issues. However, the September 23,, 2020 letter
         does not contain a timeline or any commitments to bring the City into compliance with
         State Housing Element Law for the current housing element cycle nor does it provide a
         strategy to achieve compliance with State Housing Element Law for the 6th housing
         element cycle, which is due October 15, 2021.

         As stated in HCD’s August 24, 2020 Notice of Noncompliance, HCD has determined
         that the City of Huntington Park has failed to comply with Government Code section
         65585 by failing to submit a compliant adopted housing element, which was due for the
         5th housing element cycle on October 15, 2013. Pursuant to Government Code section
         65585, subdivision (j), HCD has notified the Office of the Attorney General of the
         violation and is working with that office in anticipation of your response to this letter.

         HCD remains committed to assisting the City in meeting its housing obligations for both
         the 5th and 6th housing element cycles. To that end, HCD strongly recommends the
         City apply for a Local Action Planning Grant (LEAP). The LEAP program provides over-
         the-counter grants complemented with technical assistance to local governments for
         the preparation and adoption of planning documents, and process improvements that
         accelerate housing production and facilitate compliance to implement the sixth-cycle
         Regional Housing Needs Assessment. Jurisdictions must submit their applications by
         January 31, 2021. Information on this program can be found at
         https://www.hcd.ca.gov/grants-funding/active-funding/leap.shtml. Huntington Park’s
         maximum grant amount is $150,000. To date, HCD has received no indication that the
         City intends to apply for LEAP funds.
Sergio Infanzon, City Manager
