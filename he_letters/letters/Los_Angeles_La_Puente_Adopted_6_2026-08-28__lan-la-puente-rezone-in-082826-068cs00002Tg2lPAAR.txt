          RE: City of La Puente – Successful Completion of Required Rezones

          Dear Bob Lindsey:

          Thank you for submitting the City of La Puente’s (City) Ordinance Number 26-996,
          received for review on June 30, 2026 and adopted June 23, 2026, and Ordinance
          Number 26-998, received for review on August 26, 2026 and adopted August 25, 2026
          (Zoning Documents), to the California Department of Housing and Community
          Development (HCD). Pursuant to Government Code sections 65585, subdivisions (h)
          and (i), HCD is reporting the results of its review regarding the completion of Program
          3a (Inventory of Sites for Housing/Rezone), Program 3c, Program 4 (Mixed-Use
          Development in the Downtown Business District), and Program 6 (Mixed-Use Zoning).

          HCD is pleased to find the City’s housing element substantial in compliance with
          Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq) as of the date of this letter. This
          finding is based on HCD’s review of the Zoning Documents. HCD finds the City has
          satisfied the requirements described in HCD's October 14, 2025 letter, thereby
          completing required rezoning actions in Program 3a, Program 3c, Program 4, and
          Program 6.

          Background

          On October 15, 2025, HCD found that the City had failed to implement the required
          rezone actions in Program 3a, Program 3c, Program 4, and Program 6 included in its
          housing element pursuant to Government Code section 65583, and that this failure
          brought the City’s housing element out of substantial compliance with State Housing
          Element Law. Consequently, HCD revoked its finding of compliance for the City’s
          housing element, as authorized by Government Code section 65585, subdivision
          (i)(1)(B), and stated this revocation would remain in effect until the City completed
          required rezone actions in the aforementioned programs.
Bob Lindsey, City Manager

On June 30, 2026, HCD received Ordinance Number 26-996 which adopted the zoning
ordinance amendments to address rezone sites in Program 3a, Program 3c, and
Program 4. On August 26, 2026, the City Submitted Ordinance Number 26-998 which
adopted the zoning ordinance amendments to address rezone sites in Program 6.

Conclusion

HCD congratulates the City on the successful completion of required rezone actions in
Program 3a, Program 3c, Program 4 and Program 6. While the adopted element now
addresses the statutory requirements described in HCD’s June 24, 2025 letter by
implementing these programs, the City must continue timely and effective
implementation of all housing element programs including, but not limited to, the
following:

   •   Program 3a (Adequate Sites)
   •   Program 3c (By-Right Approval)
   •   Program 4 (Mixed-Use Development in the Downtown Business District)
   •   Program 5 (Facilitate Redevelopment)
   •   Program 6 (Mixed-Use Zoning)
   •   Program 8 (Assist Affordable Housing Development)
   •   Program 11 (Zoning Code Amendments)
   •   Program 12 (Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU))
   •   Program 14 (Fair Housing)

The City must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City now meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.
