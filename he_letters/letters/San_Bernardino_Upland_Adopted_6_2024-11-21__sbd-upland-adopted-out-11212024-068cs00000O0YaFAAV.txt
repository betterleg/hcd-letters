         RE: City of Upland’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of Upland’s (City) housing element adopted
         September 23, 2024 and received for review on September 25, 2024. Pursuant to
         Government Code section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing
         and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         The adopted housing element meets most of the statutory requirements of State
         Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq). However, the housing element
         cannot be found in substantial compliance until the City has completed necessary
         rezones to address the shortfall of sites to accommodate the Regional Housing Needs
         Allocation (RHNA) pursuant to Assembly Bill 1398 (Chapter 358, Statutes of 2021).

         Pursuant to AB 1398, a jurisdiction that failed to adopt a compliant housing element
         within one year from the statutory deadline cannot be found in compliance until rezones
         to accommodate a shortfall of sites pursuant to Government Code section 65583,
         subdivision (c), paragraph (1), subparagraph (A) and Government Code section
         65583.2, subdivision (c) are completed. As more than a year has passed and rezoning
         actions in Program 6 (Adequate Sites to Accommodate Regional Housing Needs) have
         not been completed, the housing element is out of compliance and will remain out of
         compliance until these rezoning actions have been completed. Once the City completes
         the rezone, a copy of the resolution or ordinance should be transmitted to HCD. HCD
         will review the documentation and issue correspondence identifying the updated status
         of the City housing element compliance.

         Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs
         including but not limited to the following:

    •   Program 6 (Adequate Sites to Accommodate Regional Housing Needs): This
        program commits to actions to facilitate the development of affordable housing
        in the City, including developing 96 units on City-owned sites by September of
        2025.
    •   Program 8 (Reduce Potential Constraints to Housing Development): This
        program commits to processing an amendment to the Zoning Ordinance to
        eliminate use permit (Administrative Use Permit (AUP) or Conditional Use
        Permit (CUP)) requirements on residential development and establish objective
        development and design standards for projects that are eligible for ministerial
        review by December of 2024.
    •   Program 9 (Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU)): This program commits to facilitate
        ADU development in the City, including processing code amendments to ensure
        compliance with State Law by December of 2024.
    •   Program 15 (Housing Incentives): This program commits to provide incentives
        for developing affordable housing in the City, including updating its density
        bonus ordinance by October of 2025.
    •   Program 18 (Homeless Services and Facilities): This program commits to
        amending City regulations regarding emergency shelters, transitional housing,
        supportive housing and low barrier navigation centers to ensure consistency
        with State Law by December of 2025.
    •   Program 20 (Housing for People with Disabilities or Other Special Needs): This
        program commits to encourage development of housing options for persons with
        special needs. This includes creating a reasonable accommodation process
        consistent with State Law and modifying restrictive CUP requirements for group
        homes of seven or more persons by December of 2025.
    •   Program 24 (Place-Based Improvements in South Upland): This program
        commits to encourage equitable improvement and redevelopment of South
        Upland, including several grant programs for downtown revitalization and
        infrastructure improvement.

Public participation in the development, adoption and implementation of the housing
element is essential to effective housing planning. During the housing element revision
process, the City must continue to engage the community, including organizations that
represent lower-income and special needs households, by making information regularly
available while considering and incorporating comments where appropriate. Please be
aware, any revisions to the element must be posted on the local government’s website
and to email a link to all individuals and organizations that have previously requested
notices relating to the local government’s housing element at least seven days before
submitting to HCD.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
https://www.opr.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Affordable Housing and Sustainable
Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent Local Housing Allocation consider
housing element compliance and/or annual reporting requirements pursuant to
Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing element, the City meets
housing element requirements for these and other funding sources.

HCD appreciates the assistance of the City’s housing element team provided
throughout the course of the housing element review. We are committed to assisting the
City in addressing all statutory requirements of State Housing Element Law. If you have
any questions or need additional technical assistance, please contact Reid Miller, of our
staff, at Reid.Miller@hcd.ca.gov .
