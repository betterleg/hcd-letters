          Dear Lori Waterman:
          RE: Atwater’s 5th Cycle (2016-2024) Adopted Housing Element
          Thank you for submitting Atwater’s Housing Element adopted December 9, 2019 and
          received for review on March 16, 2020. Pursuant to Government Code section 65585,
          subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing and Community Development
          (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.
          HCD is pleased to find the adopted Housing Element in full compliance with state Housing
          Element law (Article 10.6 of the Government Code). The adopted element satisfies
          the statutory requirements described in HCD’s December 6, 2019, August 30, 2018,
          May 7, 2018, and September 12, 2017 review letters.
          Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider Housing Element
          compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, CalTrans Senate Bill (SB)
          1 Sustainable Communities grants; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s Affordable
          Housing and Sustainable Communities program; and the SB 2 Planning Grants as well
          as ongoing SB 2 funding consider Housing Element compliance and/or annual
          reporting requirements pursuant to Gov. Code section 65400. With Housing Element
          compliance, Atwater meets the Housing Element requirements for these funding
          sources.
          HCD appreciates the cooperation Greg Thompson, City Planner, provided throughout
          the course of the Housing Element review. HCD wishes Atwater success in
          implementing its Housing Element and looks forward to following its progress through
          the General Plan annual progress reports pursuant to Gov. Code section 65400. If
          HCD can provide assistance in implementing the Housing Element, please contact
          Paul McDougall of our staff, at (916) 263-7420.
          Sincerely,




          Zachary Olmstead
