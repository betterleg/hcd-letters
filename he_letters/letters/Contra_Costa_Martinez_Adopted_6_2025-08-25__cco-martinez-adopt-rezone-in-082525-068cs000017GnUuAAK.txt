         RE: City of Martinez’s 6th Cycle (2023-2031) Adopted Housing Element and
             Rezone to Accommodate the Regional Housing Need Allocation

         Thank you for submitting the City of Martinez’s (City) Ordinance Numbers 1464, 1472
         and 1475 (Amendments) that were received for review June 26, 2025. The
         Amendments are intended to meet statutory requirements related to rezoning to
         accommodate the regional housing need allocation (RHNA). In addition, the California
         Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) considered prior submitted
         resolutions and ordinances including Resolutions Number 145-23, 146-23 and 147-23
         and Ordinances 1448, 1451, 1452, 1453 and 1458. Pursuant to Government Code
         section 65585, the HCD is reporting the results of its review.

         HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in substantial compliance with
         State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq) as of the date of this letter.
         This finding is based on, among other things, implementation of portions of Programs
         11 (Zoning Ordinance Amendments related to RHNA) and 13 (Adequate Sites for
         Lower-Income Households) to rezone and address a shortfall of capacity to
         accommodate the RHNA and make available prior identified sites to, among other
         provisions, permit housing developments with 20 percent affordability for lower-income
         households without discretionary action and meet minimum density and residential only
         requirements.

         Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs
         including but not limited to the following:

              •   Program 1 (Partnerships for Affordable Housing)
              •   Program 2 (Affordable Housing Funding Sources)
              •   Program 3A (Public Property Conversion to Housing)
              •   Program 4 (Accessory Dwelling Units and Junior Accessory Dwelling Units)

      The City must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
      the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
      Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
      to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
      inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
      failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
      housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
      law.

      Among other programs, Programs to rezone sites are essential to housing element
      compliance. While the City has completed sufficient rezoning related to Programs 11
      and 13, some provisions do not have complete clarity and HCD reminds the City that
      local review of housing developments with 20 percent affordability must meet by-right
      requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65583.2, subdivisions (h) and (i).
      Specifically, affordability criteria should be limited to lower-income households and non-
      residential zones must allow 100 percent residential uses. The City should monitor
      compliance with the requirements of Government Code section 65583.2, subdivisions
      (h) and (i) as part of ongoing implementation of Programs 11 and 13. Finally, HCD
      encourages the City to continue rezoning additional sites as necessary to maintain
      adequate sites to accommodate the RHNA and the City should closely monitor
      development and design standards including but not limited to lot coverage, heights and
      dwelling units per building and make adjustments as appropriate to facilitate achieving
      maximum densities and address constraints to development.

      Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
      compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
      (SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
      Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent
      Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
      requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
      element, the City now meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
      sources.
