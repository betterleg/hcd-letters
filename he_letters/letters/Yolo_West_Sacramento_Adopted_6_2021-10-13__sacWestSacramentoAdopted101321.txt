         RE: West Sacramento’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of West Sacramento’s (City) housing element adopted
         July 14, 2021 and received for review on July 15, 2021. Pursuant to Government Code
         section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing and Community
         Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         The adopted element complies with the statutory requirements of State Housing
         Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code) and was found to be substantially the
         same as the revised element reviewed on June 25, 2021.

         In addition, HCD was recently made aware of non-discretionary zoning and procedures
         related to emergency shelters that was inadvertently changed to a discretionary process
         as part of a comprehensive zoning code update. In response to this change, the City
         expeditiously proposed a zoning amendment to comply with state law which was
         recently approved by the Planning Commission. Further, the City has also confirmed, in
         the interim, any application for emergency shelters would be processed in a non-
         discretionary manner in compliance with state law. Once the City has completed
         adoption procedures to more fully document compliance with state law related to non-
         discretionary zoning and emergency shelters, a copy of the appropriate documentation
         (e.g., resolution and ordinance) should be transmitted to HCD. HCD will review the
         documentation and issue correspondence re-confirming the status of the City’s housing
         element compliance.

         For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
         element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
         the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
         Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
         http://opr.ca.gov/docs/OPR_Appendix_C_final.pdf and
         http://opr.ca.gov/docs/Final_6.26.15.pdf.
Aaron Laurel, City Manager

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.

HCD appreciates the hard work and dedication Raul Huerta, Senior Program Manager,
and Elijah Ortega, Community Investment Specialist, provided throughout the course of
the housing element review. HCD wishes the City success in implementing its housing
element and looks forward to following its progress through the general plan annual
progress reports pursuant to Government Code section 65400. If HCD can provide
assistance in implementing the housing element, please contact Hillary Prasad at
Hillary.Prasad@hcd.ca.gov.
