          RE: Marysville’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Adopted Housing Element

          Thank you for submitting the City of Marysville’s (City) housing element, which was
          adopted August 17, 2021, and received for review August 23, 2021. Pursuant to
          Government Code section 65585, subdivision (b), the California Department of
          Housing and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

          On May 12, 2021, HCD found the draft housing element to meet the statutory
          requirements of State Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of Gov. Code). However, the
          adopted element has made several changes from the draft that negate HCD’s prior
          finding. For example, many programs now “consider” or will implement “to the extent
          resources are available”. The element must include a schedule of actions that have
          beneficial impacts within the planning period pursuant to Government Code section
          65583, subdivision (c). These provisions potentially arrest the implementation of the
          housing element and remove any beneficial housing impact in the planning period. As
          for another example, the element appears to have deleted commitments essential to
          addressing displacement risks. As a result of these and other changes, the adopted
          housing element does not comply with State Housing Element Law and must be
          revised and re-adopted.

          Public participation in the development, adoption and implementation of the housing
          element is essential to effective housing planning. Throughout the housing element
          process, the City should continue to engage the community, including organizations
          that represent lower-income and special needs households, by making information
          regularly available and considering and incorporating comments where appropriate.

          For your information, some general Several federal, state, and regional funding
