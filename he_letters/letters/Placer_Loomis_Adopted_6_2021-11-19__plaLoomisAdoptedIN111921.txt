         RE: Town of Loomis 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the Town of Loomis’ (Town) housing element adopted
         August 17, 2021 and received for review on August 25, 2021. Pursuant to
         Government Code section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of
         Housing and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.
         HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in substantial compliance with
         State Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code). The adopted element
         addresses the statutory requirements described in HCD’s July 12, 2021 review. HCD’s
         finding of approval is based upon, among other things, Program 11 which commits to
         rezoning (as noted below), the removal of barriers to housing for special needs groups
         and strategies that affirmatively further fair housing (AFFH). HCD specifically notes the
         following:
            •    Regional Housing Needs Allocation (RHNA) – Rezone: HCD’s determination of
                 adequate sites to accommodate the RHNA is dependent on Program 11 to
                 meet a shortfall of housing for 35 lower-income households. The high-density
                 overlay will be applied to 2.2 acres of land and will establish a minimum of 20
                 units per acre at a maximum of 25 units per acre by May 15, 2024. The high-
                 density overlay will permit owner-occupied and rental multifamily uses by right
                 (without discretionary action) for developments in which 20 percent or more of
                 the units are affordable to lower-income households.
            •    Affirmatively Furthering Fair Housing (AFFH): The element identifies several
                 programs that encourage and promote affordable housing choices and mobility
                 options. For example, Program 16 promotes and encourages the facilitation of
                 affordable housing through objective design guidelines in line with smart growth
                 principles. In addition, Programs 6 and 7 will provide incentives for affordable
                 development options, including, reduced parking standards, increased height
                 limits, density bonus provisions and expedited application permitting and
                 processing. Lastly, Program 12 promotes the construction of accessory dwelling
                 units and encourages the construction of these units in high opportunity areas.

           Ensuring the Town meets its RHNA and provides affordable housing options is
           imperative to meeting Loomis’ AFFH obligation.
       •   Special Needs Housing: The element will accommodate special needs population
           through various programs. Examples include Programs 5 and 13, which commit to
           removing constraints on housing by amending the zoning ordinance and allowing
           employee housing, residential care facilities and low barrier navigation centers per
           state law. Program 20 accommodates persons with disabilities and elderly
           population by promoting universal designs standards that encourage safe and
           accessible living spaces.
     The Town must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
     the annual progress report pursuant to Government Code section 65400. Please be
     aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority to review
     any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is inconsistent with
     an adopted housing element or housing element and other laws. This includes failure to
     implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke housing
     element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state law.
     Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
     compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
     (SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
     Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
     Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
     requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
     element, the Town meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
     sources.

     For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
     element adoption. HCD reminds the Town to consider timing provisions and welcomes
     the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
     Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
     http://opr.ca.gov/docs/OPR_Appendix_C_final.pdf and
     http://opr.ca.gov/docs/Final_6.26.15.pdf.
