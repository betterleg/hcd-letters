         RE: Housing Element Rezoning Status

         Dear Tim Chapa:

         Thank you for submitting Ordinance Nos. 2019-30 and 2019-31 adopted July 2, 2019,
         Ordinance No. 2019-01 adopted on August 15, 2019, and Ordinance No. 2019-06 adopted
         on November 21, 2019. The final document was received on December 9, 2019 for review.
         Pursuant to Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i), the California Department of
         Housing and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review. In
         addition, HCD considered comments from Central California Legal Services pursuant to
         Government Code section 65585, subdivision (c).

         HCD is pleased to find Sanger has satisfied the requirements described in HCD’s
         July 22, 2016 and May 7, June 27, and September 18, 2018 review letters. Sanger has
         rezoned sufficient and suitable sites to accommodate the 4th cycle unaccommodated
         housing need of 796 units for lower-income households. In addition, the City of Sanger
         has completed rezone actions to accommodate the 5th cycle housing need of 284 units
         for lower-income households. As a result, the March 17, 2016 adopted element
         continues to comply with State Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Government
         Code).

         Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
         compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, CalTrans Senate Bill (SB) 1
         Sustainable Communities grants; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s Affordable
         Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and the upcoming SB 2 Planning
         Grant as well as ongoing SB 2 funding consider housing element compliance and/or
         annual reporting requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a
         compliant housing element, Sanger meets housing element requirements for these and
         other funding sources.
Tim Chapa, City Manager
