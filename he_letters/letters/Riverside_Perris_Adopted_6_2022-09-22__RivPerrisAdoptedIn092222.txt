         RE: City of Perris’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of Perris’s (City) housing element adopted on January
         25, 2022 and received for review on August 17, 2022 along with technical modifications
         authorized by Resolution 5918. Pursuant to Government Code section 65585,
         subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing and Community Development
         (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in full compliance with State
         Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code) The adopted element, with
         technical modifications, addresses the statutory requirements described in HCD’s
         July 12, 2022 review.

         Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs
         included but not limited to the following:

                   •   Place-based Strategies: The City is implementing various place-based
                       strategies to promote community revitalization and conservation in specific
                       census tracts commensurate to the location and concentration of the regional
                       housing needs allocation (RHNA). This includes targeted investments and
                       actions through Programs 1.1, 2.10, 4.2, and 4.4.

                   •   Demonstrating Adequate Sites: The City has employed various strategies to
                       demonstrate adequate sites to accommodate the regional housing needs
                       allocation (RHNA) and facilitate development on the identified sites. This
                       includes promoting lot consolidation incentives for sites smaller than 0.5 acres
                       and facilitating the subdivision of sites larger than ten acres through the
                       implementation of Program 2.6 and 2.7.

In addition, Program 2.4 identifies adequate sites to accommodate the shortfall of
3,015 units for lower-income households and 1,017 for moderate-income households
by rezoning at least 273 acres by October 2024. Among other things, the program
commits to zoning with densities of 30 units per acre and permits owner-occupied and
rental multifamily uses by-right for developments in which 20 percent or more of the
units are affordable to lower-income households.

The City must monitor and report on the results of this and other programs through the
annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400. Please
be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority to
review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is inconsistent
with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes failure to
implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke housing
element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City now meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes the
opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical Advisories
issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
https://www.opr.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.
