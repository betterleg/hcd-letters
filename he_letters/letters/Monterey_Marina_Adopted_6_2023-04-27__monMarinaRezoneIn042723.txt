         RE: City of Marina’s 5th Cycle (2015 - 2023) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of Marina’s (City) February 15, 2023 correspondence
         and documentation (Ordinance 2023-01) regarding recent completion of rezoning to
         accommodate the regional housing need allocation (RHNA) for the 4th and 5th cycle of
         the housing element. The California Department of Housing and Community
         Development (HCD) has reviewed the correspondence and documentation and is
         reporting the results of its review pursuant to Government Code section 65585.

         HCD is pleased to find the City has completed actions to address requirements
         described in HCD’s February 28, 2020 review. As a result, the December 3, 2019
         adopted element now complies with State Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the
         Gov. Code).

         Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
         compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
         (SB1) Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
         Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
         Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
         requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
         element, the City now meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
         sources.

         As a reminder, the City’s 6th cycle housing element is due December 15, 2023. HCD
         encourages the City to submit a timely housing element update to meet the statutory
         deadline. HCD encourages the City to utilize various resources available through HCD
         and through the Association of Monterey Bay Area Governments to assist with the
         housing element update.
Guido Persicone, Director
