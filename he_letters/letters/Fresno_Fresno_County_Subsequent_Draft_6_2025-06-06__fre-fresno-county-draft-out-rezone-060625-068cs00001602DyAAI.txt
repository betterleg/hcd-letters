         RE: County of Fresno’s 6th Cycle (2023-2031) Revised Draft Element

         Thank you for submitting the County of Fresno’s (County) revised draft housing element
         received for review on April 7, 2025, along with revisions received on June 4, 2025. The
         revisions were made posted and made available to the public for seven days prior to
         review. In addition, the Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD)
         considered comments from Leadership Counsel for Justice and Accountability and
         Radley Reep pursuant to Government Code section 65595, subdivision (c). Pursuant to
         Government Code section 65585, the California Department of Housing and Community
         Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         The revised draft, with revisions, meets the statutory requirements that were described
         in HCD’s review on December 23, 2024. However, the housing element cannot be
         found in substantial compliance until the County has completed Program 14 (Adequate
         Sites) to rezone sites to accommodate the Regional Housing Needs Allocation (RHNA)
         pursuant to Government Code section 65583.2, subdivisions (h) and (i) (See Below).
         The housing element will substantially comply with State Housing Element Law (Gov.
         Code, § 65580 et seq) once rezoning is completed to meet all statutory requirements
         and the element is adopted, submitted to, and approved by HCD, in accordance with
         Government Code section 65585.

         A jurisdiction that did not adopt a compliant housing element within 120 days from the
         statutory deadline (December 31, 2023) must rezone sites within one year of the
         statutory deadline and meet requirements pursuant to Government Code sections
         65583, subdivision (c)(1)(A) and 65583.2, subdivisions (c), (h) and (i), as applicable. As
         this year has passed and Program 14 (Adequate Sites) has not been completed to meet
         all statutory requirements, the housing element is out of compliance and will remain out
         of compliance until the rezoning has been completed to meet all statutory requirements.
         Once the County completes the rezoning, a copy of the resolution or ordinance should

As a reminder, the County’s 6th cycle housing element was due December 31, 2023. As
of today, the County has not completed the housing element process for the 6th cycle.
The County’s 5th cycle housing element no longer satisfies statutory requirements.
HCD encourages the County to revise the element as described above, adopt, and
submit to HCD to regain housing element compliance.

Public participation is essential to the effective planning, adoption, and implementation
of the housing element. The County should continue to engage the community
throughout the process, including during and after adoption. This includes outreach to
organizations representing lower-income and special needs populations, as well as
individuals and groups who submitted comments on the housing element. The County
should ensure that information is shared consistently through a variety of outreach
methods and that public input is meaningfully considered and incorporated where
appropriate. For example, Program 1 (Regional Collaboration and Housing
Opportunities) could clearly commit to ensure the inclusion of representatives from
disadvantaged communities and special needs populations, including farmworkers.
Additionally, the Program could clearly commit to developing and implementing
actionable strategies that are directly informed by community input gathered through the
Fresno County Housing and Community Development Citizen Advisory Committee
(CAC) meetings. For another example, Program 18 (Extremely Low-Income Housing)
could include an action to explore a potential partnership with the City of Fresno to
expand its Eviction Protection Program to serve County residents. Please be advised
that any revisions to the housing element must be posted on the local government’s
website. In addition, a link to the revised document must be emailed to all individuals
and organizations that have previously requested notice regarding the housing element,
at least seven days prior to submission to HCD.

For your information, pursuant to Government Code section 65583.3, the County must
submit an electronic sites inventory with its adopted housing element. The County must
utilize standards, forms, and definitions adopted by HCD. Please see HCD’s housing
element webpage at https://www.hcd.ca.gov/planning-and-community-
development/housing-elements for a copy of the form and instructions. The County can
reach out to HCD at sitesinventory@hcd.ca.gov for technical assistance.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, Affordable Housing and Sustainable
Communities programs, and the Permanent Local Housing Allocation consider housing
element compliance and/or annual reporting requirements pursuant to Government
Code section 65400. With a compliant housing element, the County will meet housing
element requirements for these and other funding sources.
