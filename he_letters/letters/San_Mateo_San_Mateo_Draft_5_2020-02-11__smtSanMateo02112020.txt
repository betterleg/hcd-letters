        Dear Drew Corbett:

        RE: Review of Draft Amendment to the City of San Mateo’s Housing Element

        Thank you for submitting a draft amendment to the City of San Mateo’s adopted housing
        element, received for review on January 30, 2020. Pursuant to Government Code
        section 65585, subdivisions (b) and (d), the California Department of Housing and
        Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

        As you know, the City’s element was found in compliance on February 19, 2015. The
        proposed draft amendments will not adversely impact the current compliance status of
        San Mateo’s housing element. As a result, the revised element will continue to comply
        with state housing element law (Gov. Code, Article 10.6) when these revisions are
        adopted and submitted to HCD pursuant to Government Code section 65585,
        subdivision (g).

        HCD applauds the City of San Mateo’s leadership in expanding housing opportunities
        and actively addressing climate change. We wish the city success in implementing its
        housing element and look forward to following its progress through the general plan
        annual progress reports pursuant to Government Code section 65400. If HCD can
        provide assistance in implementing the housing element, please contact Gerlinde Bernd,
        of our staff, at (916) 263-5146.

        Sincerely,




        Shannan West
