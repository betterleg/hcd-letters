         RE: South Lake Tahoe 6th Cycle (2022-2027) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of South Lake Tahoe (City)housing element adopted
         June 21, 2022 and received for review on July 7, 2022. Pursuant to Government Code
         section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing and Community
         Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review. Our review was facilitated by a
         conversation on August 18, 2022 with Hilary Roverud and Zachary Thomas and
         consultants Amy Sinsheimer and Jennifer Gastelum.

         HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in full compliance with State
         Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code). The adopted element addresses
         the statutory requirements described in HCD’s May 23, 2022 review.

         Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs
         including but not limited to the following:

               •   Program 1-4 (Using Local Incentives): This program commits, by 2023, to utilize
                   existing incentives and allow for additional coverage to make achievable
                   densities to develop affordable, workforce, and other multifamily housing.
               •   Program 1-6 (Residential-Care Facilities): This program amends City and Tahoe
                   Regional Planning Agency (TRPA) regulations to allow group homes of all sizes
                   by right in all residential districts by December 2023.
               •   Program 2-7 (Homebuyer Assistance Program Administration Partnering): This
                   program will administer a first-time homebuyer and/or moderate homebuyer
                   program for residents by September 2023.
               •   Program 2-8 (Mitigation of TRPA Regulations): This program updates TRPA
                   regulations to mitigate constraints on density bonuses, maximum density, ADUs,
                   employee housing, residential care facilities, transitional and supportive housing,
                   emergency shelters, and other zoning constraints. The City will meet with TRPA
                   to complete actions annually.

    •   Program 3-8 (Multifamily Acquisition and Rehabilitation Program): The program

Please note, the element includes multiple programs with numerical objectives based
on existing available resources. While HCD respects and appreciates the City’s
approach to set targets based on existing resources, HCD also encourages the City to
aspire beyond these targets and diligently continue to seek all available resources to
best address the housing and community development needs of the City. The City
should evaluate these programs as part of the annual progress report in implementation
of the housing elements and adjust as appropriate to meet housing and community
development needs throughout the planning period.

The City must monitor and report on the results of this and other programs through the
annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400. Please
be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority to
review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
https://www.opr.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.
