          RE: City of Dana Point’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Adopted Housing Element

          Thank you for submitting the City of Dana Point’s (City) housing element adopted
          September 20, 2022 and received for review on September 23, 2022. Pursuant to
          Government Code section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of
          Housing and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

          HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in full compliance with State
          Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code). The adopted element was found
          to be substantially the same as the revised draft element that HCD’s August 25, 2022
          review determined met statutory requirements.

          The City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs including
          but not limited to the following:

               •   Program 1.1 (Adequate Sites): Among other things, this program commits to
                   conduct monitoring on mixed-use sites to determine if further incentives such
                   are needed such as modifying development standards to waive ground floor
                   residential requirement for 100 percent affordable housing by 2025. is needed to
                   achieve affordable housing Regional Housing Needs Allocation (RHNA)
                   production goals.
               •   Program 1.3 (Accessory Dwelling Units): This program commits the City to
                   encouraging and monitoring the development of accessory dwelling units
                   (ADUs), while ensuring that a portion of new ADUs constructed each year are
                   affordable to lower-income households.
               •   Program 3.9 (Site Development Process): This program commits to interview
                   development applicants when evaluating the development application process
                   and update the zone code by 2023.
               •   Program 6.2 (Affirmatively Furthering Fair Housing (AFFH)): This program
                   commits the City to a variety of actions through the implementation of a
                   comprehensive Affirmatively Furthering Fair Housing Plan. A formalized plan

   •   Program 6.6 (Residential Care Facilities, Six or Fewer): This program commits
       to amend the zoning code to clearly articulate that a conditional use permit is not
       required for group homes for six or fewer people.
   •   Program 6.7 (Residential Care Facilities, Seven or More): This program commits
       to eliminate constraints created to the development of group homes and
       residential facilities serving over six people and establish new objective
       development standards, eliminating subjective components of the current permit
       process by June of 2023.

The City must monitor and report on the results of this and other programs through the
annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400. Please
be aware, Government Code section 65585(i) grants HCD authority to review any
action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is inconsistent with an
adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes failure to implement
program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke housing element
compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state law.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
https://www.opr.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.
