         RE: Status of the City of Manhattan Beach’s Housing Element Review

         The California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) has
         received The City of Manhattan Beach’s (City) housing element adopted and received
         on September 23, 2022. HCD received the adopted housing element while the City’s
         revised draft (received August 16, 2022) was under review. As a result, HCD will
         discontinue its 60-day review of the revised draft and will begin its 60-day review of the
         adopted element, pursuant to Government Code section 65585. HCD will make its best
         effort to expedite the adopted review.

         HCD appreciates the City’s efforts on the housing element update. We are committed to
         assisting the City in addressing all statutory requirements of State Housing Element
         Law. If you have any questions, please contact Tristan Lanza, of our staff, at
         tristan.lanza@hcd.ca.gov.
