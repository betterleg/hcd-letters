         RE: County of Marin’s 6th Cycle (2023-2031) Revised Draft Amendment to the
             Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the County of Marin’s (County) revised draft amendment to the
         housing element that was received for review on January 9, 2025. Pursuant to
         Government Code section 65585, the California Department of Housing and Community
         Development (HCD) is reporting the result of its review regarding the draft amendment
         to the housing element.

         On June 19, 2023, HCD found the County’s housing element in substantial compliance
         with State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et. seq.) and as of this writing,
         the County continues to comply with State Housing Element Law. HCD also found on
         September 12, 2024 that draft amendments would not negatively impact a finding of
         substantial compliance. However, the current revised draft amendments substantially
         differ from the draft amendments reviewed on September 12, 2024 and, if adopted, the
         revised draft amendment will negatively impact HCD’s prior finding of substantial
         compliance. Specifically, promoting housing mobility (housing choices and affordability)
         throughout the County, including areas of relatively higher resource and income and
         concentrated areas of affluence, was essential to affirmatively furthering fair housing
         (AFFH) and HCD’s June 19, 2023 finding of substantial compliance. These revised draft
         amendments would delete key provisions to address constraints to multifamily
         developments and promote housing mobility to AFFH.

         At the same time, with modifications to address constraints to multifamily development
         and promote housing mobility in areas of relatively higher resource and income and
         concentrated areas of affluence, the revised draft amendment would not negatively
         impact HCD’s June 19, 2023 finding of substantial compliance. For example, the
         revised draft amendments could modify Programs 6 (Efficient Use of Mult-Unit Land)
         and 32 (Comprehensive Review of Zoning and Planning Policies). Please see Appendix
         A for suggested modifications.

With suggested modifications (Appendix A), the revised draft amendment will not
negatively impact HCD’s finding of substantial compliance with State Housing Element
Law once the revised draft amendment has been adopted, submitted, and reviewed by
HCD pursuant to Government Code section 65585.

Public participation in the development, adoption and implementation of the housing
element is essential to effective housing planning. Throughout the housing element
process, the County should continue to engage the community, including organizations
that represent lower-income and special needs households, by making information
regularly available and considering and incorporating comments where appropriate.
Please be aware, any revisions to the element must be posted on the local
government’s website and a link must be emailed to all individuals and organizations
that have previously requested notices relating to the local government’s housing
element at least seven days before submitting to HCD.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Affordable Housing and Sustainable
Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent Local Housing Allocation consider
housing element compliance and/or annual reporting requirements pursuant to
Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing element, the County meets
housing element requirements for these and other funding sources.

HCD appreciates the hard work and diligence of the County in the housing element
update and welcomes the opportunity to discuss this review and assist with complying
with State Housing Element Law. If you have any questions or need additional technical
assistance, please contact Anthony Errichetto, of our staff, at
Anthony.Errichetto@hcd.ca.gov.

                                 Appendix A
   Suggested Modifications to Program 6 (Efficient Use of Mult-Unit Land) and
     Program 32 (Comprehensive Review of Zoning and Planning Policies)


Program 6 (Excerpts with Suggested Language in Underline)

     Annually beginning in 2024, outreach to developers and property owners to
     promote multi-unit housing opportunities, with the goal of creating 800 units in
     multi-unit housing. As part of that outreach, identity constraints and opportunities
     and make changes as necessary to facilitate multi unit housing in areas of
     affluence.



Program 32 (Excerpts with Suggested Language in Underline)

     In 2024, conduct a comprehensive review of zoning and planning policies and
     provide the Board with options for implementation. In 2025, make appropriate
     revisions to remove discriminatory language and policies, including but not
     limited to, addressing constraints to multifamily development and promoting
     housing choice and affordability in higher resource areas.
