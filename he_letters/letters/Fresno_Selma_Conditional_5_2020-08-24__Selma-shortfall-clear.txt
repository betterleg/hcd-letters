         RE: Selma Housing Element Rezoning and Compliance Status

         Dear Teresa Gallavan:

         Thank you for submitting the City of Selma’s (City) Resolution No. 2020 – 53R adopted on
         August 17, 2020 along with supporting documentation. Pursuant to Government Code
         section 65585, subdivision (i), the California Department of Housing and Community
         Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         HCD is pleased to find Selma has satisfied the requirements described in HCD’s
         July 22, 2016 and May 7, June 18, August 27, and September 27, 2018 review letters.
         Selma was required to rezone to accommodate a 4th cycle planning period shortfall of
         169 units adequately zoned to develop housing for lower-income households. As a
         result, the March 21, 2016 adopted element complies with State Housing Element Law
         (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code).

         Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
         compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, CalTrans Senate Bill (SB) 1
         Sustainable Communities grants; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s Affordable
         Housing and Sustainable Communities program and ongoing SB 2 funding (Permanent
         Local Housing Allocation) consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
         requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With housing element
         compliance, the City meets the housing element requirements for these and other
         funding sources and HCD will move forward Selma’s SB 2 planning grant application.

         HCD wishes Selma success in implementing its housing element and looks forward to
         following its progress through the general plan annual progress reports required
         pursuant to Government Code section 65400. If you have questions, need additional
         information, technical assistance, or feedback contact Robin Huntley, Senior Housing
         Policy Specialist, at (916) 263-7422.
