          RE: Huntington Beach’s 5th Cycle (2013-2021) Adopted Housing Element

          Thank you for submitting the City of Huntington Beach housing element adopted
          February 3, 2020 and received for review on February 12, 2020. Pursuant to
          Government Code section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of
          Housing and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

          HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in full compliance with State
          Housing Eement Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code). The adopted element was found
          to be substantially the same as the revised draft element that HCD’s January 10, 2020
          review determined met statutory requirements. HCD’s finding was based on, among
          other reasons, the adoption of Resolution No. 2020-07 which amended the Beach and
          Edinger Specific Plan to include an Affordable Housing Overlay that accommodates
          the Regional Housing Needs Allocation (RHNA) shortfall of 413 lower-income units and
          removes governmental constraints to the development of housing pursuant to Program
          8 (Adequate Sties Program).

          Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
          compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, CalTrans Senate Bill (SB)
          1 Sustainable Communities grants; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s Affordable
          Housing and Sustainable Communities program; the SB 2 Planning Grants as well as
          ongoing SB 2 funding (Permanent Local Housing Allocation) consider housing element
          compliance and/or annual reporting requirements pursuant to Government Code
          section 65400. With housing element compliance, Huntington Beach meets the
          housing element requirements for these funding sources.
Oliver Chi, City Manager
