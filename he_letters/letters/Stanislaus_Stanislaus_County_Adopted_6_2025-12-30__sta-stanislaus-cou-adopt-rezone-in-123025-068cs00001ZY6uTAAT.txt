         RE: Stanislaus County’s 6th Cycle (2023-2031) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting Stanislaus County’s (County) housing element adopted
         December 9, 2025 and received for review on December 10, 2025. In addition, the
         California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) received
         Ordinances 1407 and 1408 and various other documents related (Rezoning
         Documents) to rezoning to accommodate the regional housing need allocation (RHNA).
         Pursuant to Government Code section 65585, HCD is reporting the results of its review.

         On September 29, 2025, HCD determined that the revised draft Housing Element met
         the statutory requirements of State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq).
         However, the element could not be found in substantial compliance until the County
         adopted the housing element and all necessary rezones to accommodate the RHNA
         and to meet statutory requirements (Program 3-1g - Shortfall Rezoning), pursuant to
         Government Code sections 65583, subdivision (c)(1) and 65583.2, subdivisions (c), (h)
         and (i). Upon review of the Rezoning Documents, HCD is pleased to find that the
         County has completed the rezoning actions necessary to comply with the applicable
         statutory requirements described above. For example, HCD understands multifamily
         developments with 20 percent affordability will be permitted without discretionary
         review. Further, necessary changes to the general plan land use map are approved and
         will be reflected in the County’s land use maps as authorized by the Board of
         Supervisors. Further, the adopted housing element is substantially the same as the
         revised draft reviewed on September 29, 2025. As a result, the adopted element and
         Rezoning Documents now substantially comply with State Housing Element Law as of
         the date of this letter.
Angela Freitas, Director

Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities program, HCD’s Prohousing
Designation Program, HCD’s Prohousing Incentive Program, and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the County now meets housing element requirements for these and other
funding sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the County to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation:
https://www.lci.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.
