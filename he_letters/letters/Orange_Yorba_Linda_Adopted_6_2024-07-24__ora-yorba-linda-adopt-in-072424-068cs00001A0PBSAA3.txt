          RE: Yorba Linda’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Amendment to the Adopted Housing
              Element

          Thank you for submitting the City of Yorba Linda’s amendment to the housing element
          which was adopted June 18, 2024 and received for review on June 19, 2024. Pursuant
          to Government Code section 65585, the California Department of Housing and
          Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

          On April 8, 2022, HCD found the City’s housing element in substantial compliance with
          State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq). On December 6, 2023 and
          February 16, 2023, the City submitted draft amendments to the adopted housing
          element. These amendments did not change the April 8, 2022 finding of substantial
          compliance with State Housing Element Law. On February 23, 2024, HCD reviewed
          the revised draft amendments submitted on February 16, 2024 and found the revised
          draft amendments do not impact the April 8, 2022 finding of substantial compliance.
          The February 23, 2024 review also found the element will continue to comply with
          State Housing Element Law once the amendments have been adopted, submitted, and
          reviewed by HCD pursuant to Government Code section 65585. As the adopted
          amendments are substantially the same as the draft amendments reviewed on
          February 23, 2024, the adopted housing element and amendments continue to
          substantially comply with State Housing Element Law. This finding of substantial
          compliance has not changed since the April 8, 2022 finding of substantial compliance
          with State Housing Element Law.

          Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs
          including but not limited to the following:

               •    Program 8 (Housing Opportunity Sites & Rezone Program)
               •    Program 9 (Affordable Housing Overlay)
               •    Program 10 (Commercial Mixed-Use Overlay)

Specifically, timely and effective implementation of programs (e.g., Programs 8, 9, 10,
11, 12 and 15) to accommodate the regional housing need allocation (RHNA) and
address governmental constraints are critical to substantial compliance with State
Housing Element Law.

The City must monitor and report on the results of these and other programs through
the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code section 65400.
Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City continues to meet housing element requirements for these and other
funding sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
https://www.opr.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.
