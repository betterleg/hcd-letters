         RE: Orinda 6th Cycle (2023-2031) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of Orinda housing element adopted January 31, 2023
         and received for review on February 1, 2023. Pursuant to Government Code section
         65585, subdivision (h), the California Department of Housing and Community
         Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review. Our review was facilitated by a
         conversation on February 28, 2023 with Drummond Buckley, Winnie Mui, and
         consultants Jennifer Gastelum and Cynthia Walsh. In addition, HCD considered
         comments from several stakeholders and members of the community and the City’s
         March 13, 2023 correspondence, pursuant to Government Code section 65585,
         subdivision (c).

         HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in full compliance with State
         Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code). The adopted element addresses
         the statutory requirements described in HCD’s January 13, 2023 review.

         For your information, HCD did not consider the CalTrans site as part of demonstrating
         adequate sites to accommodate the Regional Housing Need Allocation (RHNA) and as
         a result, the site should not be utilized for the purposes of maintaining adequate sites
         pursuant to Government Code section 65863. However, HCD supports the City’s efforts
         to further increase housing choices and affordability beyond the RHNA, including the
         CalTrans site and BART site and encourages the City to address the many comments
         and perspectives as part of the planning process.

         Additionally, the City must continue timely and effective implementation of all programs
         including but not limited to the following:

             •    Program 1.C (Caltrans Gateway Site Development): This program commits the
                  City to rezone the CalTrans Gateway site and decertify the cite to allow for
                  affordable housing development by January 2026.

   •   Program 1.D (Residential Zoning Districts and Land Use Designation): This
       program commits the City to establish two new zoning districts to encourage
       housing for lower-income households and comply with rezone requirements by
       May 2026. From the City’s March 13, 2023 correspondence, HCD understands
       the City will establish development standards, including heights, that will facilitate
       achieving maximum densities.
   •   Program 1.E (Promotion of Accessory Dwelling Units): This program commits the
       City to provide incentives to encourage ADU development throughout the City by
       December 2024.
   •   Program 1.J (Sites Used in Previous Housing Element Cycles): This program
       commits the City to rezone sites identified in previous housing elements to
       comply with by-right requirements when at least 20 percent or more of the units
       are affordable to lower income households by January 2026.
   •   Program 1.L (Lot Consolidation): The program commits the City to provide
       incentives for lot consolidation by January 2024.
   •   Program 3.A (Shared Housing Program): This program commits the City to
       participate in a shared housing program to provide housing opportunities
       throughout the City by December 2024.
   •   Program 4.A (Amend Development Standards to Remove Constraints): The
       program commits the City to revise the zoning code to comply with state law
       regarding emergency shelters, multifamily housing, employee housing, low-
       barrier navigation centers, single room occupancy, residential care facilities, and
       transitional and supportive housing by December 2024.
   •   Program 4.E (Revised Parking Standards): The program commits the City to
       reduce parking requirements by December 2024.
   •   Program 5.A (Affirmatively Furthering Fair Housing (AFFH)): The program
       commits to numerous actions to AFFH including outreach, promoting housing
       mobility, and work with housing providers annually throughout the planning
       period and by December 2023.

In addition, the element includes Programs 1.A (Downtown Precise Plan) and 1B
(Housing Opportunity Sites) identifying adequate sites to accommodate the shortfall by
committing to rezone at least 60 acres to RH, RVH, RH-25, and RH-40 zones by May
31, 2026. Among other things, the program commits to zoning with densities of 20-30
and 20-50 units per acre and permit owner-occupied and rental multifamily uses by right
for developments in which 20 percent or more of the units are affordable to lower
income households.

Finally, among other factors, the City is entirely the highest resource category in access
to opportunity, wholly a concentrated area of affluence and predominantly consists of
households with the highest median income, a stark contrast to the rest of the region.
These conditions and circumstances warrant significant and robust actions (not limited
to the RHNA) to promote housing mobility and increasing housing choices and
affordability throughout and beyond the City, including in lower-density neighborhoods.
While the element includes various programs to promote housing mobility, these actions

are marginally sufficient and further geographic targeting is limited to high resource
areas which can be anywhere in the City. As part of implementation, HCD encourages
the City to aggressively pursue numeric targets and geographic targets throughout the
City, including lower density neighborhoods. In addition, the City must report on

targeted funding or other strategies to encourage affordability) to further promote

The City must monitor and report on the results of programs described above and other
programs through the annual progress report, required pursuant to Government Code
section 65400. Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i)
grants HCD authority to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it
determines is inconsistent with an adopted housing element or housing element law.
This includes failure to implement program actions included in the housing element.
HCD may revoke housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not
comply with state law.

As noted in the element, the City lacks a buffer of site capacity to accommodate its
lower-income RHNA. Pursuant to Government Code section 65863, local governments
must ensure the inventory of sites accommodate the regional housing need by income
throughout the planning period of the element. In addition, no local government action
shall reduce, require or permit the reduction of the residential density for any parcel, or
allow development of any parcel, at a lower residential density by income category than
identified in the site inventory or program unless the local government makes written
findings. The required findings must demonstrate the reduction is consistent with the
adopted general plan, including the housing element and the remaining sites identified
in the housing element are adequate to accommodate the jurisdiction's share of the
regional housing need.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City now meets housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the City to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Planning and Research at:
https://www.opr.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.
