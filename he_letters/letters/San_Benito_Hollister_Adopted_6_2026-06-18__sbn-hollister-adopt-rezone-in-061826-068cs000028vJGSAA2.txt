         RE: City of Hollister’s 6th Cycle (2023-2031) Adopted Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of Hollister’s (City) housing element that was adopted
         on April 20, 2026 and received for review on May 8, 2026. In addition, California
         Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) received Zoning
         Ordinance Amendment 2026-2, Planning Commission Resolution Number 2026-04,
         Ordinance Number 1274, Ordinance Number 1275, Ordinance Number 1276,
         Resolution Number 2026-48, and Resolution Number 2026-49 and various other related
         documents (Rezoning Documents) related to meeting rezoning and accommodating the
         regional housing needs allocation (RHNA). Pursuant to Government Code section
         65585, HCD is reporting the results of its review. In addition, HCD considered
         comments from 5th Street Development pursuant to Government Code section 65585,
         subdivision (c). The review was expedited to meet deadlines in the stipulated judgement
         between the City and HCD and enable the jurisdiction to meet funding criteria for the
         Permanent Local Housing Allocation Program.

         On February 13, 2026, HCD found the City’s revised draft housing element met the
         statutory requirements of State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq.).
         However, the housing element could not be found in substantial compliance until the
         housing element was adopted and the City had completed necessary rezones and met
         all statutory requirements pursuant to Government Code sections 65583, subdivision
         (c)(1)(A) and 65583.2, subdivisions (c), (h) and (i).

         HCD is pleased to find the adopted housing element in substantial compliance with
         State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq) as of the date of this letter.
         The adopted element was found to be substantially the same as the revised draft
         element that HCD’s February 13, 2026, review determined met statutory requirements.
         Further, the City has completed rezoning actions to address the requirements that were
         described in HCD’s February 13, 2026 review. Specifically, HCD finds the Rezoning
         Documents meet statutory requirements pursuant to Government Code sections 65583,
         subdivision (c)(1) and 65583.2, subdivisions (h) and (i).

Please be aware, Government Code section 65585, subdivision (i) grants HCD authority
to review any action or failure to act by a local government that it determines is
inconsistent with an adopted housing element or Housing Element Law. This includes
failure to implement program actions included in the housing element. HCD may revoke
housing element compliance if the local government’s actions do not comply with state
law.

For your information, the City received a Stipulated Final Judgment and Order
(Stipulated Judgment) for failing to adopt a housing element revision that substantially
complies with Housing Element Law within 120 days of the statutory deadline
(December 15, 2023). HCD reserves the right to enforce any and all rights and
remedies provided in the Stipulated Judgment.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant, the Affordable Housing and Sustainable
Communities program, and HCD’s Permanent Local Housing Allocation consider
housing element compliance and/or annual reporting requirements pursuant to
Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing element, the City now
meets housing element requirements for these and other funding sources.

HCD appreciates the hard work and cooperation of the City housing element team
throughout the housing element update and rezoning processes. HCD wishes the City
success in implementing its housing element and looks forward to following its progress
through the general plan annual progress reports required pursuant to Government
Code section 65400. If HCD can provide assistance in implementing the housing
element, please contact Andrea Grant, of our staff, at andrea.grant@hcd.ca.gov.
