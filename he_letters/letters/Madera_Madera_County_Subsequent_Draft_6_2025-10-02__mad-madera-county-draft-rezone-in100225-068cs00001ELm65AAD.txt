         RE: Madera County’s 6th Cycle (2024-2031) Housing Element and Rezones to
             Accommodate the Regional Housing Need Allocation (RHNA)

         Thank you for submitting the County of Madera’s (County) draft resolutions and
         ordinances (Zoning Documents) related to rezoning to make prior identifies sites
         available and accommodate the regional housing need allocation (RHNA). The Zoning
         Documents were received for review on September 25, 2025 and October 1, 2025.
         Pursuant to Government Code section 65585, the California Department of Housing
         and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         On June 27, 2025, HCD found the revised draft housing element met the statutory
         requirements of State Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq). However, the
         housing element could not be found in substantial compliance until the County has
         adopted the housing element and completed necessary rezones to make prior identified
         sites available and accommodate the RHNA to comply with various statutory
         requirements. Please see HCD’s June 27, 2025 review for additional information. HCD
         has reviewed the draft Zoning Documents and is pleased to find the draft Zoning
         Documents meet the statutory rezoning requirements described above, particularly
         pursuant to Government Code sections 65583, subdivision (c)(1) and 65583.2,
         subdivisions (c), (h) and (i). The housing element will substantially comply with State
         Housing Element Law (Gov. Code, § 65580 et seq) when rezones are completed and
         submitted to meet all statutory requirements and the element is adopted, submitted to
         and approved by HCD, in accordance with Government Code section 65585.

         As a reminder, the County’s 6th cycle housing element was due January 31, 2024. As
         of today, the County has not completed the housing element process for the 6th cycle.
         The County’s 5th cycle housing element no longer satisfies statutory requirements.
         HCD encourages the County to expeditiously complete rezones, adopt, and submit to
         HCD to regain housing element compliance.
Jamie Bax, Deputy Director

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the County will meet housing element requirements for these and other
funding sources.

For your information, some general plan element updates are triggered by housing
element adoption. HCD reminds the County to consider timing provisions and welcomes
the opportunity to provide assistance. For information, please see the Technical
Advisories issued by the Governor’s Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation at:
https://www.lci.ca.gov/planning/general-plan/guidelines.html.

HCD appreciates the responsiveness and dedication of the County’s housing element
team during the update and review. HCD looks forward to receiving the County’s
adopted housing element and rezone documents. If you have any questions or need
additional technical assistance, please contact Laurissa Wells, of our staff, at
Laurissa.Wells@hcd.ca.gov.
