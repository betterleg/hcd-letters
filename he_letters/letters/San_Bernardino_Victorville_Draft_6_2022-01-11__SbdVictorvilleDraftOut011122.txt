         RE: Review of City of Victorville’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Draft Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the City of Victorville’s (City) draft housing element received
         for review on November 12, 2021, along with revisions on January 6, 2022. Pursuant to
         Government Code section 65585, subdivision (h), the California Departmentof Housing
         and Community Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review.

         The draft housing element with revisions addresses most statutory requirements
         described in HCD’s, September 17, 2021 review; however, additional revision is
         necessary to fully comply with State Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov.
         Code), as follows:

             Affirmatively further fair housing in accordance with Chapter 15 (commencing with
             Section 8899.50) of Division 1 of Title 2. (Gov. Code, § 65583, subd. (c)(10)(A).)

             Goals, Actions, Metrics and Milestones: As noted in the prior review, goals and
             actions must include metrics toward meaningful impact to affirmatively further fair
             housing (AFFH). While in some cases programs do include metrics, programs,
             including metrics, should be explicit to AFFH to target significant and meaningful
             outcomes that overcome identified patterns and trends. For example, programs
             related to safe housing conditions, rehabilitation, improving community
             infrastructure, revitalization and quality of life could be targeted, including metrics,
             toward areas of higher need, such as areas of high segregation and poverty.
             Programs related to promoting housing choices, new development or creating new
             affordable opportunities could be targeted (not solely) toward areas of higher
             opportunity. Finally, programs should explicitly reflect the City’s efforts toward broad
             community revitalization, including implementation of the Old Town Specific Plan.

         The element will meet the statutory requirements of State Housing Element Law once it
         has been revised and adopted to comply with the above requirement.

Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the City will meet housing element requirements for these and other funding
sources.
