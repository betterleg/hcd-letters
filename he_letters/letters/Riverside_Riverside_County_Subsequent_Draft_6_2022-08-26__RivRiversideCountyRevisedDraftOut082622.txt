         RE: Riverside County’s 6th Cycle (2021-2029) Revised Draft Housing Element

         Thank you for submitting the County of Riverside’s (County) revised draft housing
         element received for review on June 29, 2022. Pursuant to Government Code section
         65585, subdivision (b), the California Department of Housing and Community
         Development (HCD) is reporting the results of its review. In addition, HCD considered
         comments from California Rural Legal Assistance, Inc. (CRLA), Leadership Counsel for
         Justice and Accountability, Public Interest Law Project (PILP), and the Sierra Club
         pursuant to Government Code section 65585, subdivision (c).

         The revised draft housing element addresses most statutory requirements described in
         HCD’s December 30, 2021 review; however, additional revisions are necessary to fully
         comply with State Housing Element Law (Article 10.6 of the Gov. Code), as follows:

             1. Affirmatively further[ing] fair housing in accordance with Chapter 15
                (commencing with Section 8899.50) of Division 1 of Title 2…shall include an
                assessment of fair housing in the jurisdiction. (Gov. Code, § 65583, subd.
                (c)(10)(A).)

                  Promote and affirmatively further fair housing opportunities and promote housing
                  throughout the community or communities for all persons regardless of race,
                  religion, sex, marital status, ancestry, national origin, color, familial status, or
                  disability, and other characteristics... (Gov. Code, § 65583, subd. (c)(5).)

                  guide the appropriate programmatic response. The analysis should still discuss
                  changes over time, comparisons to other neighborhoods, effectiveness of past

      Specific Plans and Affordability: The element must still clarify if the Valente
      Specific Plan is being used as an approved project or in the sites inventory.
      Please refer to HCD’s previous review.

      Valente and Travertine Specific Plans: As noted in the prior review, the element
      now rescales pending affordability assumptions between lower and moderate-
      income households. For your information, these assumptions must be utilized for
      the purposes of complying with No Net Loss Law pursuant to Government Code
      section 65863.

      Manufactured Homes: While the element now includes additional data on trends,
      it must still incorporate land and financing costs, including availability of financing
      for lower-income households and rescale affordability assumptions.

      Electronic Sites Inventory: For your information, pursuant to Government Code
      section 65583.3, subdivision (b), the County must utilize standards, forms, and
      definitions adopted by HCD when preparing the sites inventory. Please see
      HCD’s housing element webpage at https://www.hcd.ca.gov/community-
      development/housing-element/index.shtml for a copy of the form and
      instructions. The County can reach out to HCD at sitesinventory@hcd.ca.gov for
      technical assistance. Please note, upon re-adoption of the housing element, the
      County must submit an electronic version of the sites inventory with its adopted
      housing element to sitesinventory@hcd.ca.gov.

      Programs: As noted described above, the element does not include a complete
      site analysis, therefore, the adequacy of sites and zoning were not established.
      Based on the results of a complete sites inventory and analysis, the County may
      need to add or revise programs to address a shortfall of sites or zoning available
      to encourage a variety of housing types.

      In addition, Program H-2 (Infrastructure Availability and Coordination) includes
      some actions such as developing analysis, conducting studies, meeting and
      identifying disadvantaged communities but it should also include specific
      commitments to target meaningful outcomes. In addition, as noted in the prior
      review, the Program should be modified based on lessons learned from the prior
      planning period and should consider the various related public comments.

The element will meet the statutory requirements of State Housing Element Law once it
has been revised and re-adopted to comply with the above requirements.
As a reminder, the County’s 6th cycle housing element was due October 15, 2021. As
of today, the County has not completed the housing element process for the 6th cycle.
The County’s 5th cycle housing element no longer satisfies statutory requirements.
HCD encourages the County to revise the element as described above, adopt, and
submit to HCD to regain housing element compliance.
Several federal, state, and regional funding programs consider housing element
compliance as an eligibility or ranking criteria. For example, the CalTrans Senate Bill
(SB) 1 Sustainable Communities grant; the Strategic Growth Council and HCD’s
Affordable Housing and Sustainable Communities programs; and HCD’s Permanent
Local Housing Allocation consider housing element compliance and/or annual reporting
requirements pursuant to Government Code section 65400. With a compliant housing
element, the County will meet housing element requirements for these and other
funding sources.
We are committed to assist the County in addressing all statutory requirements of State
