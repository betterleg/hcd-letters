# Standard language in HCD's housing element review letters

From 2,151 letters with text and 52,431 paragraphs of 20+ words, by `scripts/letter_patterns.py`. Names, dates and numbers are replaced with <JUR>, <DATE> and <N> so letters to different places compare as equal. A *group* is paragraphs that share at least half their 5-word phrases; a *variant* is a tighter group (85%) inside it. Groups in fewer than 25 letters are left out. Data: [`patterns.csv`](patterns.csv).
