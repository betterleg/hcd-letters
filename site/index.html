What HCD requires

Housing element review letters, read finding by finding. Each finding is filed under the statutory requirement HCD quotes above it (Gov. Code § 65583 or § 65588) and tagged with HCD's topic label. Round is the letter's place among a jurisdiction's reviews in that cycle. Counts leave out HCD's standard sentences, so they reflect what HCD wrote for each place.
